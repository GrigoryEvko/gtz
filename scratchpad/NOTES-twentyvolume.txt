THE 1/5 WITNESS, EXACT

Let C be the symmetric Paley conference matrix of order six, C = C^T, zero
diagonal, entries +-1 off it, C^2 = 5I.  Put P = (I + C/sqrt 5)/2.  Then P^2 = P
to 5.6e-17, trace P = 3, every diagonal entry is 1/2 and every squared
off-diagonal entry is 1/20.  Hence all fifteen delta and all fifteen delta' equal
1/5 EXACTLY and both totals are 3.  This is the icosahedron frame: six equiangular
lines in R^3 with cos^2 = 1/5.  It is NOT a tie -- at uniform weight
max_T lambda_min(P[T,T] - diag t_T) = +0.10973, and in atom coordinates
lambda_min(S_T - 1) = +0.658 at the triples with positive three-cycle.

So 1/5 is attained and cannot be improved, and the pair carrying it is as far
from parallel as a (6,3) design allows.

JOB C -- THE MEASUREMENT, AND ITS NEGATIVE ANSWER

CHART.  Do the search in M = P - diag(t), NOT in atom coordinates.  A triple T
dominates exactly when M[T,T] is PSD, because S_T >= I iff W W^T >= I with
W = diag(1/sqrt t_T) V_T iff P[T,T] >= diag(t_T).  Every entry of M is O(1) and
no weight is ever inverted.  A sibling lane found that computing
lambda_min(S_T - 1) directly MANUFACTURES ties: at weights near 1e-11 the
roundoff is 4e-6, larger than the reported gap.  The M chart is immune.

    mu := min over the fifteen pairs of det P[{p,q}]   -- a function of P ALONE
    F  := max over the twenty triples of lambda_min(M[T,T])
    design is an exact TIE  <=>  F = 0
    THE HINGE AT (6,3) IS EXACTLY:  F = 0  implies  mu = 0.

degeneration is t_c -> 0 with t_c l_c -> 1/2: the atom does NOT drop out, it
becomes an infinitely heavy direction of vanishing weight.

WHICH CONSTRAINT DOES THE WORK.  Neither of the two the brief named.
  * NOT realisability.  The sibling lane built the matroid pattern -- four
    coplanar plus two generic, simple, rank three, delta'_{56} = 0, mu = 0.054 --
    and it IS a design for every positive t on the simplex.  Realisability is free.
  * NOT the tie condition on its own.  It is satisfiable in the limit.
  * IT IS THE STRICT POSITIVITY OF THE WEIGHTS.  Every near-counterexample lives
    on the boundary of the weight simplex and the achievable |F| tracks the floor.

CONSEQUENCE, and it is a NO-GO.  No certificate for the hinge with an ABSOLUTE
constant can exist.  The margin must be proportional to the smallest weight,
which is the campaign's named universal obstruction measured once more, this time

the best absolute-constant statement of its kind -- SHARP at 1/5 -- and it can
never reach 0, so it cannot close the hinge by itself.  Do not spend a cycle
trying to push the constant 1/5 down by a global argument.

WHY the boundary is where the counterexample lives: the hinge is FALSE at (5,3),
and a (6,3) design with a degenerating weight approaches the (5,3) cell.  The
diamond is sitting on that boundary.
