trace(adj G) is e_{k-1}(G), the total of the principal minors of order k-1.  At
rank three these are the campaign's pairGapMinor_eq_pairMinorTotal_mul_reading_*
and det_add_atomMatrix_of_unit_null, both proved there by explicit
linear_combination certificates on the NINE entries.  That proof does not
generalise: at rank k the entries number k^2.

The proof here is structural and rank-free:
  (i)   a unit kernel vector makes det G = 0, so G * adj G = 0;
  (ii)  hence every column of adj G is a kernel vector;
  (iii) corank one makes each column a multiple of w, so adj G = w c^T;
  (iv)  adj of a symmetric matrix is symmetric, forcing c in R w;
  (v)   the trace names the scalar.
No eigenvalue, no characteristic polynomial, no certificate.

("every kernel vector is a multiple of w") rather than through rank vocabulary.

F-R4C-2.  THE FIRST RANK-FOUR BOUNDARY SYSTEMS.
-----------------------------------------------
Nobody had produced one.  Method: search the boundary locus as the ZERO SET of
  Phi(D) = sum over k-subsets T of max(lmin(S_T - I), 0)^2
under a hard weight floor t >= 0.03, never by minimising max_T lmin (which
converges to dust).  Parametrisation: weights by softmax over the floor,
directions by an arbitrary m x k matrix whitened against the weighted frame
operator, so Parseval holds exactly by construction at every iterate.

Harness: Julia, 300 threads, BLAS pinned to one thread, hand-rolled in-place
cyclic Jacobi for the 4x4 symmetric eigenproblem, allocation-free hot loop, no
lock (restarts chunked, each task owns its workspace).
CALIBRATION: the Jacobi agrees with LAPACK to 7.55e-15 over 5000 random
symmetric 4x4 matrices.
Exact check first: the uniform graphic design of K5 at (10,4) has every leverage
equal to 4 and all 65 of its dominating 4-sets STRICT — not a boundary system.

F-R4C-3.  PRIMITIVITY IS WHAT BLOCKS IT.  THE EVIDENCE FOR CONJECTURE 9.2.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same search with collinearity forbidden by a barrier on cos^2 <= 1 - delta,

  (10,4)  0.02    1.3684e-05      +3.188e-03      0.9800

READING.  With collinearity allowed the search drives max lmin to ~1e-6 and the
minimiser is collinear at every cell.  With collinearity forbidden max lmin
STAYS STRICTLY POSITIVE, two to four orders of magnitude higher, at every cell
and at both barrier strengths — the objective never approaches zero.  A
primitive rank-four boundary system was not reachable at any window cell, while
a collinear one was reachable at all three.  That is exactly what conjecture 9.2
asserts, and it is the first evidence of any kind for the band axiom.

HONEST LIMITS, stated because the campaign's record demands it:
  - the free search reaches max lmin ~1e-6, not an exact boundary system; these
    are near-boundary points, not certified ties;
  - the barrier figures are PENALISED, and this campaign has a recorded incident
    of a penalty trapping an optimiser into a false report.  The separation here
    is 2 to 4 orders of magnitude and consistent across three cells and two
    barrier strengths, which is why it is reported, but it is evidence and not
    proof;
  - the minimisers sit at a weight-floor boundary with one dominant weight, so
    they probe the corner of the domain, not its interior.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT.  A certified rank-four boundary system, obtained by
solving the active-set system exactly at one of these near-boundary points
rather than by descent, and then a census of its collinear pairs.  The active
set at (8,4) is the 8 subsets listed above.

F-R4C-5.  PROCESS.
------------------
Two runaway Julia processes were created and killed during this round: the first
searches used g_tol = 1e-14 with FINITE-DIFFERENCE gradients, which never
converges, so every restart burned its full iteration budget — 14 minutes on one
cell.  Setting g_abstol = 1e-10 and pinning BLAS to a single thread (Julia
threads times BLAS threads was oversubscribing 384 cores at 523 threads per
process) brought the same three cells to 114, 172 and 293 seconds.
RULE: with finite-difference gradients, never set a gradient tolerance below
about 1e-10, and always pin BLAS to one thread when the hot loop is hand rolled.
