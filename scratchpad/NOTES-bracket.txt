3. THE CORNER OBSTRUCTION
-------------------------
At a corank-two corner C = {x,y,z} the transport collapses:
    heavyExcess(x) * P_yz = P_xy * P_xz          (from the axis readings)
    sum over a of t_a [xya][xza] = P_yz          (the corner transport)
    sum over a of t_a [xya]^2 = 1 + e_x + e_y    (the corner cross-axis budget)
Multiply the three base readings at one atom and use the landed corner fact
P_xy P_xz P_yz >= 0:  the three quantities P_yz*M^x_a, P_xz*M^y_a, P_xy*M^z_a
CANNOT all be nonnegative unless [xya][xza][yza] = 0.  So some base disagrees in
sign with the pairing it sums to, and then the transport supplies a second atom
of the opposite sign, and the floor fires.

    alone cannot cut.  What cuts is the MASS equation the three bits satisfy.

5. THE RESIDUAL
---------------
The floor is a real theorem violated by the complex witness, but its
hypothesis-free corollary at a corner is an EXISTENTIAL ("some base has an
opposite-sign pair, hence a large informative bracket"), and the witness

opposite-sign pair.  From the sign coupling: the number of coherent bases is 0, 1
or 3 and 3 is impossible, so at least two bases carry one.  The witness carries
exactly two.  THE GAP IS ONE BASE.
