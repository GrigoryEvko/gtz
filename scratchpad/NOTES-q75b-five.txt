Q7.5(b) AT (5,3), AND THE VOLUME CHART AT (6,3)
===============================================

VERDICT ON THE ASSIGNMENT
-------------------------
Question 7.5(b) at (5,3) is NOT closed.  It is very probably TRUE under STRICT
heaviness, and it is FALSE without it.  Three findings, in order of weight.

1. HEAVINESS IS LOAD-BEARING, AND IT IS NOT FREE.
   Landed: Gtz.exists_inadmissible_nonparallel_of_leverage_le_one.
   At ANY (m,3) design an atom with 0 < l_a <= 1 has a partner b with
   q_ab <= 0 and squared area w_ab > 0.  So the unqualified Q7.5(b) is FALSE
   at every design carrying a unit-length atom.  The landed
   Gtz.leverage_one_le_of_isTie_fiveThree gives 1 <= l_c at a (5,3) tie for
   free; the strict form 1 < l_c is exactly what the question needs and
   exactly what is not free.  Proof is two lines from the new squared-area row
   law sum_b t_b w_ab = 2 l_a.

2. THE QUESTION HAS A PRICE INSIDE EVERY WEAK DOMINATOR.
   Landed: Gtz.pairGapMinor_pos_inside_dominator_of_q75b.
   A pair inside a weak dominator is admissible non-strictly, so if it were
   inadmissible it would have q = 0, hence squared area l_a + l_b - 1 > 1 > 0,
   hence NOT parallel.  So Q7.5(b) at a strictly heavy design IMPLIES that no
   weak dominator carries a vanishing pair minor -- every weak dominator has
   gap rank two with no vanishing reading.  That is the standing hypothesis of
   the survey's rank-two section, and it is a CONSEQUENCE of the question, not
   an aid to it.  A successor must not assume it while proving the question.

3. THE MARGIN VANISHES WITH THE SMALLEST WEIGHT.  MEASURED.
   Minimise q_01 over (5,3) designs subject to: no triple dominates strictly,
   every leverage at least 1.02, every weight at least tau, normalised squared
   area w_01/(l_0+l_1-1) at least 0.15.  Nelder-Mead, Stiefel x simplex chart,
   64 threads, 192-384 random starts, three descent passes each.
     tau = 0.12 -> min q_01 = +0.2887  (tie residual 5.4e-6)
     tau = 0.05 -> min q_01 = +0.0901  (tie residual 1.6e-9)
     tau = 0.01 -> min q_01 = +0.0218  (tie residual 5.2e-8)
   The minimum never reaches zero and shrinks about linearly, min q_01 ~ 2 tau.
   Dually, minimising the tie residual under q_01 <= 0 and an area floor:
     tau = 0.10 -> 6.3e-3 ;  0.05 -> 3.5e-3 ;  0.02 -> 1.5e-3 ;  0.01 -> 3.9e-4.
   With NO weight floor the search reaches tie residual 1.4e-7 carrying
   q_01 = -0.12 and squared area 2.49 at weights
   (0.189, 0.665, 4e-7, 0.147, 3e-7).  The counterexample exists ONLY in the
   limit where a weight vanishes.  Any certificate for Q7.5(b) at (5,3) will
   therefore carry a margin proportional to the smallest weight.  This is the
   campaign's universal obstruction seen from the counterexample side.

WHERE THE NUMBER SIX ENTERS
---------------------------
The volume budget has C(m,3) terms, so one is at least 1/C(m,3); three weights
never multiply to more than 1/27.  The floor is 27/C(m,3), which exceeds one
exactly when C(m,3) < 27, that is exactly when m <= 6:
  C(4,3)=4, C(5,3)=10, C(6,3)=20, C(7,3)=35.
This is the first instrument in the campaign whose size threshold is m <= 6 on
the nose.  The exact floor is 1/e3(t) with e3(t) the third elementary symmetric
function of the weights; at uniform weight and m=5 that reads 12.5, which is
ATTAINED at four of the diamond's ten triples, so the budget argument is sharp
even though the constant 27/C(m,3) is not.

THE (6,3) BRIDGE FROM MY ASSIGNMENT TO THE MAIN TARGET
------------------------------------------------------
Gtz.exists_parallel_pair_of_q75b_of_gapDet_pos: at a strictly heavy tie, ONE
triple with positive gap determinant plus Q7.5(b) produces a parallel pair,
which is the survey's hinge for that design.  The Sylvester chain is ordered
(heaviness, then the pair minor, then the gap determinant), so at a tie with
heaviness and a positive gap determinant the MIDDLE link breaks -- and the
chain reads through any of the three pairs, so ALL THREE pair minors of that
triple are nonpositive.
Contrapositive, proved: at a strictly heavy tie with every pair admissible
(branch B), EVERY gap determinant is nonpositive, so every squared volume is
capped by W - L + 1.  Against the volume floor this forces branch B at (6,3) to
carry a triple with W >= L + 7/20.

ROUTE THAT DIED, WITH ITS WITNESS
---------------------------------
The weighted gap-determinant budget cannot produce a positive gap determinant:
at the (5,3) diamond sum_S (prod t) D_S = (0*8 + (-10)*2)/125 = -0.16 < 0.  So
"some triple has D > 0" does not follow from the budget, and the bridge above
needs its hypothesis supplied elsewhere.

WHAT A SUCCESSOR MUST BOUND
---------------------------
For Q7.5(b) at (5,3): the quantity that is unbounded is the LEVERAGE.  Every
counterexample the search found sat at a vanishing weight, hence at an
unbounded leverage l_c = s_c/t_c.  A certificate must either cap the leverage
in terms of the other data or accept a margin proportional to min_c t_c.  The
three landed caps -- the area floor, the area ceiling and the triple floor --
are all weight-quadratic and all degenerate as a weight goes to zero, which is
consistent with the measurement and is why none of them closes the question.
