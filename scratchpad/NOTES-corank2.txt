2. THE LEDGER IDENTITY (the new mathematics)
--------------------------------------------
Fix a pair f != h. For each x outside the pair, the refusal slack is

  slack_x = (1-P_hh) P_fx^2 + 2 P_fh P_fx P_hx + (1-P_ff) P_hx^2
            - (1-P_xx) * Delta ,      Delta = (1-P_ff)(1-P_hh) - P_fh^2 .

The refusal of the triple {f,h,x} complement says `0 <= slack_x` when the pair is
admissible. The four slacks, weighted by the co-weights, sum EXACTLY to the
weighted ghost deficit:

  sum over x not in {f,h} of (1 - t_x) * slack_x = ghostDeficitForm D f h .

At general size the two sides differ by `(m - 6) * Delta`, so the identity is
exact at six atoms and at no other size. At five atoms the slack budget is one
Delta larger, which is the room the diamond needs.

Ingredients: three instances of `pivot_coweight_projection` and one instance of
`coweight_pivot_deficit`. Nothing else.

F41-1. THE NEW INSTRUMENT: PARSEVAL AT THE ADJUGATE PROBE (`b75e5aa`)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
For a triple (a,b,c) and coefficients w, set

  zeta(w) = w0*(b x c) + w1*(c x a) + w2*(a x b) .

Each atom of the triple reads it back DIAGONALLY: a.zeta = w0*[abc], and
likewise for b, c. So the three inside terms of Parseval decouple and the
remaining atoms only help. Dropping them:

  **[abc]^2 * (t_a w0^2 + t_b w1^2 + t_c w2^2)  <=  zeta(w).zeta(w)**

`Gtz.design_adjugate_probe_law`. NO tie, NO corner, NO domination hypothesis --
it holds at every weighted design and every triple. Its w = e_a instance is the
landed pair-mass floor `t_a [abc]^2 <= w_bc`; its OFF-DIAGONAL content is new.

F41-2. THE CORNER CAPS (`ca694c8`, `0a59579`)
----------------------------------------------
At a corner the landed rank-one Gram collapses every cross-product pairing:
  pair area   w_ef = l_e + l_f - 1 = 2 + lam - l_g   (`corner_inside_pairArea_eq`)
  mixed cross (g_z x g_x).(g_x x g_y) = -(g_y.g_z)   (`corner_crossPair_collapse`)
Feeding these into the probe law gives, AT EVERY CORNER (no Z1, both horns too):
  DIAGONAL   l_x + (1+lam)*t_x <= 2 + lam            (`corner_leverage_weight_cap`)
  OFF-DIAG   (g_y.g_z)^2 <= (2+lam-l_y-(1+lam)t_y)(2+lam-l_z-(1+lam)t_z)
  SIMPLEX    e_y(1-t_z) + e_z(1-t_y) <= (1+lam)(1-t_y)(1-t_z),  e = l - 1
and on Z1 the sharp scalar form
  t_y*l_y + t_z*l_z <= 1 + (1+lam)*t_y*t_z           (`corner_oneAxisZero_weight_law`).
