CORRECTIONS TO THE BRIEF
  Part 2 (a).  "If p_1 = 0 then g_1 parallel g_2" is FALSE as stated.  (B) gives
     t_2 alpha_2 p_2 = 0, and the branch alpha_2 = 0 puts g_2 in the plane, so
     FIVE atoms are coplanar and the two off-plane atoms need not be parallel.
     The correct statement is a disjunction: p_1 = 0 forces g_1 parallel g_2 OR
     g_2 flat.  The brief's own prose says the same thing after the claim.
  Part 4 wall.  The brief's "t_2 alpha_2^2 (t_3+t_4+t_5+t_6) <= t_1 p_1^T A^-1 p_1"
     is CORRECT, but only in case (b), and it is the collapse of a two-term law.
     The general law is
        (t_3+t_4+t_5+t_6) <= t_1 s_1 + t_2 s_2,     s_i = q_i^T A^-1 q_i,
     equivalently, cleared of the inverse,
        frameBlockDet aa bb cc * (t_3+t_4+t_5+t_6)
            <= t_1 frameAlpha(q_1) + t_2 frameAlpha(q_2).
     In case (b), q_2 = -mu q_1 with mu = t_1 alpha_1 / (t_2 alpha_2), so
     s_2 = mu^2 s_1 and t_1 s_1 + t_2 s_2 = t_1 s_1 / (t_2 alpha_2^2) by (A),
     which is the brief's form.
  The wall is STRICTLY STRONGER than the landed area ceiling here, and that is
     the point of coplanarity.  Summing the gate over the whole complement of
     the pair reproduces Gtz.crossNormSq_le_of_isTie exactly.  Summing it over
     the TWO OFF-PLANE atoms only drops four nonpositive terms from the left and
     four nonnegative terms from the right, because the other two flat atoms
     have alpha = 0.  Larger left side, smaller right side.
  Part 4 criterion.  Sherman-Morrison is right but the matrix inverse is not
     needed.  det(gap of {i,a,b}) = det(A) (alpha_i^2 - 1) - q_i^T adj(A) q_i,
     a polynomial identity, and adj(A) form IS Gtz.frameAlpha.  So the criterion
     is exactly  frameBlockDet * (alpha_i^2 - 1)  vs  frameAlpha, no division.

SECOND PASS — the plane weight deficit, and what it kills
=====================================================================

THE ENGINE.  A plane Parseval family (sum over a finite support of t_a g_a g_a^T
= I_2, with the total weight sigma FREE) whose total weight is BELOW ONE carries
a pair that beats every nonzero plane probe.  Proof: rescale the atoms by
sqrt(sigma) and the weights by 1/sigma, which is a genuine rank-two design; spend
the landed Gtz.gtz_rank_two; read the weak domination back through the stretch.
The deficit is exactly what turns <= into <.  At sigma = 1 the conclusion is
false, and that boundary is the landed rank-two tie classification.
  Gtz.exists_planeStrict_of_weightSum_lt_one, every size.

KILL 1 (unconditional).  All but one atom of a rank-three design coplanar.  The
exceptional atom is a spike, its shadow vanishes, the rest carry the whole plane
on weight 1 - t, the engine gives a STRICT plane pair, and the gap of that pair
with the spike is block diagonal with both blocks positive.  So the triple
dominates strictly and the design is NOT a tie.  Frame-free readings: no atom of
a rank-three tie is orthogonal to every other atom, and no plane holds five atoms
of a (6,3) tie.
  Gtz.not_isTie_of_spike, Gtz.not_isTie_of_atom_orthogonal_to_all.
  MEASURED: 27028 designs at m = 4,5,6,7 with m-1 coplanar atoms, EVERY one has a
  strictly dominating triple, no exceptions.

KILL 2 (unconditional).  Only the vanishing SHADOW is needed, not the flatness of
the others: an atom parallel to the axis contributes nothing to the in-plane
identity whatever the rest do.  Gtz.exists_planeStrictPair_of_zeroShadow.

THE LAW (unconditional, and the strongest thing here).  Every nonzero atom is
parallel to ONE axis: its own.  So for EVERY atom of EVERY rank-three design,
two OTHER atoms strictly over-cover that atom's orthogonal plane.  The tree's two
existing routes to a plane-strict pair each pay — four pairwise transversal atoms,
or the Nesterenko threshold 1 + t < 2 t l.  This one pays nothing.
  Gtz.exists_frame_planeStrictPair_orthogonal_to_atom.
  MEASURED: 20000 whitened designs at m = 4..8, 120000 atom-planes, ZERO
  failures, worst margin +0.0215 (at m = 4).
  CONSEQUENCE at (6,3): the landed pair budget and the landed area window fire at
  EVERY atom of EVERY tie, with the transversality census removed.
  Gtz.sixThree_tie_pairBudget_at_every_atom,
  Gtz.sixThree_tie_crossNormSq_window_at_every_atom.

THE FOUR-COPLANAR COLLAPSE (conditional).  The two off-plane shadows are parallel
(the two-plane structure), so their two rank-one terms add to ONE rank-one term
along a unit direction carrying their total shadow mass.  Replacing them by one
fictitious unit atom of that mass leaves a FIVE-member plane family of weight
(coplanar weight) + t_1 lambda_1 + t_2 lambda_2, below one exactly when the two
off-plane shadows are LIGHT on weighted average.  Cauchy-Schwarz keeps the
fictitious unit atom out of the returned pair, so both members are COPLANAR.
  Gtz.exists_coplanar_planeStrictPair_of_lightOffPlane.
  So residue (i) of the coplanar bridge implies residue (ii), in weighted-average
  form.  The gap left is: some shadow light, but the weighted average not light.

four-coplanar stratum (whitening parametrisation, so Parseval and coplanarity are

positive.  No four-coplanar tie was found and none was excluded.  Do NOT claim the
coplanar bridge is vacuous.
