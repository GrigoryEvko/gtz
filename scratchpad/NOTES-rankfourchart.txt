NOTES - RANK FOUR: THE TWO REGISTRY HINGES ON THE DEPENDENCY SPACE
==================================================================
Lane: the two registry axioms that live at rank four and up.
  Skeleton.obligationSubThresholdBandHinge            band, 2k <= m < k(k+1)/2
  Skeleton.obligationThresholdCellHingeRankFourAndUp  cell, m = k(k+1)/2, k >= 4
At rank four the band is the two sizes eight and nine. The threshold cell is
size ten. Nothing below proves either axiom. Both stay open.


F-R4D-0.  THE ANSWER IN ONE LINE
---------------------------------
The hinge at every cell is a statement about ONE subspace of R^size and one
positive weight vector. The dependency space carries the hypothesis and the
conclusion at the same time: a tie is a pair of quadratic-form comparisons on
that subspace, and a parallel pair is a nonzero vector of that subspace carried
by two coordinates.


F-R4D-1.  A PARALLEL PAIR IS A SPARSE DEPENDENCY
-------------------------------------------------
  Gtz.hasParallelPair_iff_hasSparseDependency

    HasParallelPair D  <->  some nonzero z with sum_c z_c g_c = 0 has support 2.

Both directions, at every size and every rank. A parallel pair g_q = r g_p gives
the dependency e_q - r e_p. A dependency on {p,q} gives the pair, and a zero atom
gives the zero ratio the campaign's predicate already allows.

THIS IS THE CHART FORM OF THE HINGE'S CONCLUSION that the brief asked for. The
determinant reading Gtz.ChartCore.HasParallelPairCore names one 2x2 minor at a
time. This names the object: the dependency space meets a coordinate 2-plane.

  Gtz.dominates_iff_dependencyPairMass          (subset-indexed)

    Dominates D C  <->  for every dependency z ,
        sum_c z_c^2/(1 - t_c)  <=  sum_{c not in C} z_c^2/(t_c (1 - t_c)) .

The left side does NOT mention C. So a rank-subset is decided by ONE fixed
positive form on the dependency space, tested against the sum of the rank-one
forms of the CORANK labels it omits. At corank one that sum has one term. At
corank two it has two.

AND THE LEFT SIDE IS THE WEIGHT-AVERAGE OF THE RIGHT-HAND FAMILY:
  Gtz.sum_weight_mul_pairMass     sum_c t_c K_c = N ,  K_c(z) = z_c^2/(t_c(1-t_c)) ,
                                  N(z) = sum_c z_c^2/(1-t_c) .
That identity IS the engine of the corank-one rigidity, in dependency currency: a
family that never passes its own weighted mean is constant. At corank two the
right side has two terms and the averaging stops. That is exactly why corank one
is rigid and corank two is not.

F-R4D-4.  THE SELF-DUAL CELL: SEVENTY TESTS, THIRTY-FIVE BUDGETS
------------------------------------------------------------------
  Gtz.dependencyDefect D C z  :=  sum_{c not in C} z_c^2/t_c - sum_{c in C} z_c^2/(1-t_c)
  Gtz.dependencyDefect_add_compl

    defect(C, z) + defect(C^c, z) = sum_c z_c^2 (1 - 2 t_c)/(t_c (1 - t_c)) .

The right side does not know which pair it came from. At size = 2*rank -- the
band's LOWER ENDPOINT, and the first rank-four band cell (8,4) -- the complement
of a rank-subset is a rank-subset, so the C(2k,k) tests pair up. At (8,4) the
seventy tests carry thirty-five budgets.

Two consequences, both landed:
  Gtz.dependencyDefect_pos_of_compl_neg
      with no weight past one half, two COMPLEMENTARY selections cannot fail at
      the same dependency
  Gtz.not_dominates_and_compl_of_budget_neg
      a dependency that reads the budget negatively forbids a complementary
      dominating pair outright.

The weight enters only through the pair mass t_c(1-t_c) and the sign 1 - 2 t_c.
A negative budget needs a label of weight past one half, and at most one label of
a design can carry that.

MEASURED at (8,4): 60 random designs, all seventy subsets, residual 8.9e-15.

F-R4D-6.  JOB D: CORANK TWO IN NORMAL FORM
--------------------------------------------
Landed, frame-free:
  Gtz.corankTwo_dominates_iff, Gtz.corankTwo_posDef_gap_iff
      at size rank+2 a rank-subset omits a pair {d,e} and the criterion tests N
      against the two pair-mass forms K_d and K_e
  Gtz.exists_pair_compl_of_card_eq_corankTwo
  Gtz.corankTwo_not_dominates_of_sparse
  Gtz.not_dominates_of_dependency_inside
      a nonzero dependency INSIDE a selection refutes domination outright. One
      line from the criterion, and it recovers Gtz.not_dominates_of_exists_null
      and Gtz.not_dominates_of_parallel_within with no null probe and no
      determinant.

DERIVED AND MEASURED, NOT PROVED IN LEAN. Normalise the criterion by N. Put
K'_c = N^{-1/2} K_c N^{-1/2} = lambda_c u_c u_c^T with |u_c| = 1. Then

  sum_c t_c lambda_c u_c u_c^T = I_2 ,   hence   sum_c t_c lambda_c = 2 ,

and, with th_de the angle between u_d and u_e,

  univ \ {d,e} DOMINATES  <->  lambda_d lambda_e sin^2(th_de) - lambda_d - lambda_e + 1 >= 0
                               AND  lambda_d + lambda_e >= 2 ,
  STRICTLY                <->  the two inequalities are strict.

So a corank-two design is m unit directions on a circle, m scalars lambda_c >= 0,
and m weights, under ONE planar tight-frame constraint. A parallel pair is a line
through the origin that holds all but at most two of the live directions.

At corank ONE the same normalisation gives sum_c t_c lambda_c = 1 and the
criterion lambda_d >= 1, so no strict dominator forces every lambda_c = 1. That
is the landed corank-one rigidity, recovered.

At corank TWO the trace budget is 2 and the pair test is lambda_d + lambda_e >= 2
PLUS the angle term. A pair with lambda_d + lambda_e above 2 still fails when its
two directions are near-parallel. THAT is the escape that breaks the averaging
argument, and it is why corank two carries parallel-free ties while corank one
does not.

MEASURED: 9600 pairs over the cells (4,2), (5,3), (6,4), (7,5) and (8,6). Zero
weak mismatches, zero strict mismatches. Frame residual 1.7e-15, trace residual
2.2e-15.


F-R4D-7.  JOB C: THE (8,4) CRITERION, WRITTEN DOWN
----------------------------------------------------
A design at (8,4) IS a pair (Dep, t): a four-dimensional subspace of R^8 and a
positive weight vector on the simplex. Every such pair is a design and every
design is such a pair, because the atoms are recovered from Dep^perp by the
Parseval whitening, which is always solvable at a positive t.

Take a basis Z of Dep. For a 4-subset S put

  X_S := Z^T diag(e_S) Z ,   e_S,c = 1/t_c for c in S ,  -1/(1-t_c) otherwise.

Then the 4-subset  univ \ S  dominates iff X_S >= 0 and dominates strictly iff
X_S > 0. Seventy 4x4 forms on one 4-space, and three laws relate them:

 (a) X_S + X_{S^c} = Z^T diag((1-2t_c)/(t_c(1-t_c))) Z ,  the SAME for all S.
     Measured residual 8.9e-15.
 (b) det X_S = sum_T (prod_{c in T} e_S,c) det(Z_T)^2  by Cauchy-Binet, the sum
     over all seventy 4-subsets T. Measured residual 1.8e-12.
 (c) sum_c t_c K_c = N , the weight-average identity. Measured residual 4.5e-16.

The Pluecker coordinates det(Z_T) are, up to sign, the atom brackets of the
COMPLEMENTARY 4-subset, so (b) prices every selection determinant by the seventy
squared brackets of the design. MEASURED, and it is an EQUALITY and not merely a
proportion once both bases are orthonormal: |det(Z_T)| = |det(G_Tc)| at every one
of the seventy subsets, residual 8.9e-16 over the cells (8,4), (7,3) and (6,4).

CENSUS, 400 random (8,4) designs, exact statement of what was counted:
  dominating 4-subsets      min 1, median 8, max 19  of 70
  strictly dominating       min 1, median 8, max 19  of 70
  designs with none         0
  ordered complementary pairs both dominating   min 0, median 0, max 10

At a random design domination and strict domination always agreed, so the tie
locus has measure zero, as expected.

F-R4D-9.  WHAT THE FIELD DOES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
------------------------------------------------------
Every criterion in this lane is FIELD-BLIND. The strict criterion divides by
sqrt(scale) and reads probe^2. Over C the same three lines run with Complex.normSq
and nothing changes. So the realness obstruction of
Gtz/Complex/ComplexHingeRefutation.lean does NOT enter the criteria, and it does
not enter the sparse-dependency reading either: a complex parallel pair is a
complex dependency of support two, by the same two lines.

CONSEQUENCE, and a successor must not miss it: the dependency reading ALONE can
never close either registry axiom. The hinge is false over C at (4,2) and (6,3),
and every statement this lane landed survives complexification. Whatever closes
the axioms has to spend the real-only fact somewhere the dependency chart cannot
see it.


F-R4D-10.  ROUTES CLOSED, AND ROUTES OPEN
--------------------------------------------
CLOSED here: nothing. This lane closed no route and refuted no statement.

OPEN, and the two worth a successor:

 1. THE BUDGET AT THE SELF-DUAL CELL. Gtz.dependencyDefect_add_compl pairs the
    seventy (8,4) tests into thirty-five. What is NOT known is whether a tie can
    put both members of a pair on the boundary at once. If it cannot, the tie
    locus at (8,4) is cut by thirty-five conditions and not seventy, and the
    band's first cell becomes a smaller object than anyone has treated it as.

 2. THE CORANK-TWO NORMAL FORM. F-R4D-6 turns corank two into m circle
    directions, m scalars and one tight-frame constraint. Mechanising the
    N-normalisation costs a square root of a 2x2 form, which Mathlib supplies
    through the continuous functional calculus. With it, the corank-two tie
    stratum becomes a finite explicit system, and the question "does a
    parallel-free corank-two tie exist at rank four" becomes a question about
    six directions on a circle.

DO NOT SPEND A CYCLE ON: an averaging proof of corank-two rigidity. F-R4D-6
names the exact escape, the near-parallel direction pair, and the numerics of
F-R4D-8 confirm that corank two carries parallel-free ties in abundance while
corank one carries none.
