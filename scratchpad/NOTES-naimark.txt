N-1  THE CORRECTION TO Q75A-8.
-----------------------------
Q75A-8 records  tripleGapDet_C <= 0  <==>  det(Omega_{C'} - Omegabar) <= 0.
THAT STATEMENT IS WRONG AS WRITTEN.  The identity is missing the identity:

  tripleGapDet_C = (prod_{c in C} (1/t_c - 1)) * det(1 + Omega_{C'} - Omegabar)

with Omega_c = Lambdatilde_c Lambdatilde_c^T/(1 - t_c), Omegabar = sum_c t_c Omega_c.
Without the 1 the right side is (-1)^{m-k} det(sum_{c in C} t_c Omega_c), which is
sign-forced and carries no information.  Verified to 2.3e-13 at (6,3), (5,3),
(4,3), (7,3), (6,4), (8,3), (7,4).  LANDED as Gtz.tripleGapDet_omega_law and
Gtz.tripleGapDet_naimark_law.  The prefactor is strictly positive
(Gtz.naimark_gapDet_prefactor_pos), so the sign statement of Q75A-8 is correct
once the identity is put back: Gtz.tripleGapDet_nonpos_iff_naimark.


N-2  THE MASTER IDENTITY, AND WHY NO SQUARE ROOT IS NEEDED.
-----------------------------------------------------------
A Naimark dual frame is m vectors co_c in R^r, r = m - k, with
   sum_c t_c co_c g_c^T = 0        (dependency)
   sum_c t_c co_c co_c^T = 1       (dual Parseval)
Put Phi = [G | co] of shape m x (k + r) and Psi = Phi^T diag(t).  The three
design laws say exactly Psi Phi = 1 block by block, so Phi Psi = 1, which is

   Gtz.naimark_gram_identity :   t_d (g_c . g_d + co_c . co_d) = delta_cd .

Everything else in the four modules is a reading of that one line.  No square
root, no rank argument, no spectral theorem.  Consequences, all landed:
  * co_c . co_d = -(g_c . g_d) for c != d.  Cross norms are duality invariants;
    every Bargmann 3-cycle is NEGATED.
  * t_c(l_c + ltilde_c) = 1, so ltilde_c = 1/t_c - l_c and t_c ltilde_c = u_c.
  * (co, t) is a weighted design of size m and rank m - k with the SAME weights,
    and the construction is an involution up to the Gram.
  * sum_c t_c ltilde_c = m - k.


N-3  DOMINATION HAS THREE EQUIVALENT FORMS.
--------------------------------------------
For an injective pick of k atoms, all landed and all checked with ZERO
mismatches against domination on 2975 selections over 105 designs:

  (a) Gram_C >= 1                                  (primal, k x k)
  (b) coGram_C <= diag((1 - t_c)/t_c)              (dual cap, k x k)
  (c) Reading(C) := sum_{c in C} (t_c/(1-t_c)) co_c co_c^T <= 1     (r x r)
  (d) Reading(C^c) >= Omegabar - 1 = sum_c t_c M_c (complement, r x r)

(b) is Gtz.naimark_dominates_iff_coGram_le, (c) is
Gtz.naimark_dominates_iff_reading_le, (d) is
Gtz.naimark_dominates_iff_compl_reading.  (c) uses only a DIAGONAL square root
plus the landed rectangular transfer of Gtz/LinAlg/PsdKit.lean.


N-4  THE SHARP DESIGN: DOMINATION COMPLEMENTS ITSELF AT ARBITRARY WEIGHTS.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Omegabar - 1 = sum_c t_c M_c is POSITIVE DEFINITE (Gtz.naimarkSharpTarget_posDef;
smallest eigenvalue 0.0173 over the measured run).  Whitening it gives

  Gtz.exists_naimark_sharp_design :  every (m,k) design D carries an (m, m-k)
  design E with the SAME weights such that a k-selection dominates in D exactly
  when its complementary (m-k)-selection dominates in E.

At (6,3) both are (6,3) designs.  The landed
Gtz.dominates_iff_dominates_chartDual_compl_of_uniformWeight_sixThree is the
UNIFORM WEIGHT case of this.  The weights are now arbitrary and carried
unchanged.  Consequence Gtz.gtzWeighted_six_three_iff_dominating_compl reads the
open cell as "every (6,3) design has a triple whose COMPLEMENT dominates the
sharp design".


N-5  THE HEAVY COUNT IS SHARPER THAN THE LANDED ONE, BY EXACTLY ONE ATOM.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gtz.rank_sub_one_sub_threshold_le_card_heavySet gives k - 1 heavy atoms.  The
correct count is k:

  Gtz.rank_le_card_heavyLeverage :  k <= card {c : 1 <= l_c} .

Proof: k = sum_c t_c l_c splits over the light set L = {l_c < 1} and its
complement.  On L each term is STRICTLY below t_c, and sum_L t_c <= 1, while off
L each term is at most 1.  The strictness turns k <= 1 + card into k < 1 + card,
and both sides are integers.  The landed proof used <= and lost the atom.

At rank three this is the difference between "at least two heavy atoms" and "a
whole triple".  Reading it in the dual gives Gtz.corank_le_card_cappedLeverage:
at least m - k atoms have t_c(l_c + 1) <= 1, the diagonal condition a capped
selection needs.  At (6,3) both counts are exactly three
(Gtz.sixThree_candidate_counts).

THE SQUEEZE.  A dominating triple T of D satisfies T inside heavyLeverageSet D
(Gtz.dominates_subset_heavyLeverage) and T^c inside heavyLeverageSet E for the
sharp design E (Gtz.sixThree_dominating_squeeze).  So every dominating triple
sits between two explicit sets.  If D has exactly three heavy atoms the triple is
FORCED to be them (Gtz.sixThree_forced_triple), and (6,3) at that design is a
single yes-or-no question.


N-6  THE REALNESS EQUATION AT SIX POINTS -- IT IS REAL-ONLY.
-------------------------------------------------------------
The bracket law (Gtz.naimark_bracket_law), proved from dual Parseval and NOT
from Jacobi's complementary minor identity, reads

   (prod_{c in C} t_c) det Gram_C = (prod_{c not in C} t_c) det coGram_{C^c} .

At (6,3) both sides are 3 x 3.  Expanding the dual determinant and using
co_c . co_d = -(g_c . g_d) pins the Bargmann 3-cycle -- Gtz.sixThree_bargmann_cycle:

   2 p_ab p_ac p_bc . (t_a t_b t_c)
     = (t_a t_b t_c)(ltilde_a ltilde_b ltilde_c - ltilde_a q_bc - ltilde_b q_ac
                     - ltilde_c q_ab)  -  (t_x t_y t_z) [x y z]^2

for the complementary triples {a,b,c} and {x,y,z}.  Squaring gives
Gtz.sixThree_bargmann_realness, whose right side is 4 q_ab q_ac q_bc.

FIELD CHECK, PASSED.  Over C the master identity and the cycle equation STILL
HOLD (residuals 4.9e-14 and 2.6e-10 over 400 complex (6,3) designs), because the
left side becomes 2 Re(cycle).  But the SQUARED form fails: the slack
4 q_ab q_ac q_bc - (...)^2 ranged over [1.4e-9, 1.2e4] on 8000 complex triples
and is exactly 0 over R.  The squared form is the first realness-consuming
EQUATION on a (6,3) design.  A proof of it must fail over C, and it does.

At m = 4 the same machinery collapses: the dual is one-dimensional, the cycle
becomes a square, and Gtz.corankOne_threeCycle reproduces the landed four-atom
identity t_a t_b t_c p_ab p_ac p_bc = -(u_a u_b u_c) AT EVERY RANK, not only at
rank three.  Gtz.corankOne_bracket gives t_a t_b t_c [a b c]^2 = 1 - t_d l_d.


N-7  DUALITY REMOVES THE CYCLE FROM THE DETERMINANT.
------------------------------------------------------
The 3 x 3 gap determinant has exactly one term odd in the off-diagonal entries.
Duality negates exactly those, so the primal and dual gap determinants of the
same triple ADD to a cycle-free polynomial -- Gtz.naimark_gapSum_cycleFree:

   det(Gram_T - 1) + det(coGram_T - 1)
     = prod (l_c - 1) + prod (ltilde_c - 1) - sum (1/t_c - 2) q .

No relation between size and rank is needed and the dual need not be a design.
Residual 6.1e-11 over 125 designs at (6,3), (5,3), (7,3), (4,3), (8,3).

At (6,3) the bracket law removes the cycle from the PRIMAL determinant alone --
Gtz.sixThree_tripleGapDet_cycleFree, residual 9.5e-11.

The pair minors go the same way: Gtz.naimark_pairGapMinor_sub says the DIFFERENCE
of the primal and dual pair minors is free of the cross reading altogether,
q_ab - q*_ab = (l_a-1)(l_b-1) - (ltilde_a-1)(ltilde_b-1).


N-8  WHAT A SUCCESSOR MUST BOUND.
----------------------------------
The route stops where every other lane stops, at the same wall and for the same
reason, and the wall now has a name in this vocabulary.

The complementation (d) turns domination into
   Reading(C^c) >= sum_c t_c M_c ,
a strictly positive convex combination of the very family being selected from.
At corank one the M_c are SCALARS, the argmax of M_c beats the convex
combination, and a dominating selection exists.  At corank three the M_c are
rank-one 3 x 3 matrices and the argmax argument has no analogue: three
rank-one matrices summing above a positive definite average is not implied by
any single maximum.  THAT is the exact quantity a successor must bound:

   min over triples C of  lambda_min( Reading(C^c) - (Omegabar - 1) ) ,

and it must be bounded below by zero over all (6,3) designs.  It is scale-free
in the weights only through Omegabar.  The margin of every certificate built so
far is proportional to the smallest weight because Omegabar blows up as t_c -> 1,
which is the closed form of the universal obstruction in these coordinates:
t_c/(1 - t_c) is the coefficient of M_c and it is unbounded on the open simplex.

Three concrete openings, in order of cheapness:

  (i)  The squeeze of N-5 leaves at most C(card heavyLeverageSet D, 3) candidate
       triples and forces one when the count is three.  MEASURE the distribution
       of card heavyLeverageSet over a directed exact hunt.  If it is three on
       the hard locus the cell reduces to one determinant question.
  (ii) The cycle-free reading of N-7 makes tripleGapDet a polynomial in
       leverages, weights, SQUARED pair readings and one squared bracket.  Every
       term except the leverage products has a forced sign.  A positivity
       certificate in that basis does not have to reason about the 3-cycle sign
       at all, which is what killed the earlier attempts.
  (iii) The realness equation of N-6 is the only landed statement that is FALSE
       over C.  Any total instrument must consume it.  It expresses one squared
       bracket in terms of leverages, weights and pair squares of the
       COMPLEMENTARY triple, which is a relation the elimination fronts never
       had.

NOT TRIED, AND WORTH ONE ROUND: is the sharp map an involution on the (6,3) cell
up to the Gram?  If D and its double sharp have the same Gram, the counterexample
class is closed under an involution that complements triples, and a parity or
fixed-point count may bite.  The Naimark involution itself is FIXED-POINT FREE on
(6,3): a self-dual design would need Gram = diag(1/(2t)), which is diagonal of
rank six while the Gram has rank three.
