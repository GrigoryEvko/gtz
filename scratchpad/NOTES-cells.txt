F48-1.  CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF: THE CELL-H KILL NEEDS  A_z NOT PosDef
=======================================================================
The brief said "the three outside floors kill cell H".  Measured, that is only
true WITH the cell-H hypothesis.  Exact construction (Parseval residual 4e-15,
1.55M feasible samples):
   A_y > 0 alone            : all three outside floors hold at 1,005,671
                              of 1,549,540 samples  (65 percent!)
   A_y > 0 AND A_z not PD   : all three floors hold at 0 of 19,847
So `A_z not PosDef' is load-bearing, not decoration.  Mechanism, proved by hand:
   W := sum_{d in K} g_d g_d^T - 1  satisfies  W <= A_z, so A_z not PD => W not PD.
   A_y = W + g_y g_y^T > 0 and W not PD  =>  W has EXACTLY ONE nonpositive
   eigenvalue (a rank-one update fixes at most one), and
        W not PD  <->  det W <= 0  <->  g_y^T A_y^{-1} g_y >= 1  (the y-floor).
So cell H carries all FOUR floors, and the y-floor is free from A_z.

ALSO: the pure four-set question "A > 0 with all four triples failing" has
abundant solutions -- take a tight frame with sum g_c g_c^T = (1+eps) I, then
A = eps*I > 0 and each triple det = eps^2 (eps - |g|^2) < 0.  So the corner is
genuinely needed; no 4-set-only argument can work.
