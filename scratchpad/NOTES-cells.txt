F48-1.  CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF: THE CELL-H KILL NEEDS  A_z NOT PosDef
=======================================================================
The brief said "the three outside floors kill cell H".  Measured, that is only
true WITH the cell-H hypothesis.  Exact construction (Parseval residual 4e-15,
1.55M feasible samples):
   A_y > 0 alone            : all three outside floors hold at 1,005,671
                              of 1,549,540 samples  (65 percent!)
   A_y > 0 AND A_z not PD   : all three floors hold at 0 of 19,847
So `A_z not PosDef' is load-bearing, not decoration.  Mechanism, proved by hand:
   W := sum_{d in K} g_d g_d^T - 1  satisfies  W <= A_z, so A_z not PD => W not PD.
   A_y = W + g_y g_y^T > 0 and W not PD  =>  W has EXACTLY ONE nonpositive
   eigenvalue (a rank-one update fixes at most one), and
        W not PD  <->  det W <= 0  <->  g_y^T A_y^{-1} g_y >= 1  (the y-floor).
So cell H carries all FOUR floors, and the y-floor is free from A_z.

ALSO: the pure four-set question "A > 0 with all four triples failing" has
abundant solutions -- take a tight frame with sum g_c g_c^T = (1+eps) I, then
A = eps*I > 0 and each triple det = eps^2 (eps - |g|^2) < 0.  So the corner is
genuinely needed; no 4-set-only argument can work.

F48-3.  THE PAIR CONSTRAINT AT A NON-DOMINATING DESIGN  (derived, exact)
=======================================================================
det(S_{ab} - 1 + g_a g_a^T) = -(2 q_ab + x_b),  x_c := l_c - 1  (rank-2 formula),
so the per-pair law plus "every triple through {a,b} fails" gives, at every
ADMISSIBLE HEAVY pair,
      2 * q_ab * (t_a + t_b)  <=  x_a (1 - t_b) + x_b (1 - t_a).
MEASURED: this slack is NEGATIVE (i.e. the producer FIRES) at 118,487 of 120,000
random m=6 designs, and descent drives it to -1.7e13.  Correct behaviour: almost
every design dominates.  TIES are exactly where the constraint holds.
=> The constraint alone CANNOT prove the theorem, because ties satisfy it.
   Recorded so nobody re-derives it hoping otherwise.

F55-1.  THE PROMOTION EXISTS IN THE ATOM-FIXED SHAPE (commit 1c90e0c)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The coordinator asked whether a promotion lemma exists between the two index
shapes.  IT DOES.  The cell-H trio is the STAR of the fixed atom:
    {x,c,d}, {x,b,d}, {x,b,c}
and every one of those triples contains a pair THROUGH x.  So the corner arm's
per-triple promotion applies to each with a pair the fixed atom belongs to:
    {x,c,d} via (x,c),   {x,b,d} via (x,b),   {x,b,c} via (x,b).
TWO admissible pairs through the fixed atom promote the WHOLE trio -- not three,
and not the outside pairs at all.  Landed as
    Gtz.star_exists_posDef_of_exists_gapDet_pos     (two pairs, minimal)
    Gtz.star_exists_posDef_of_admissibleStar        (the full star)
    Gtz.cellH_exists_dominating_triple              (chained against the trio)
plus the raw-vector Gram bridge Gtz.atomTriple_posDef_iff_tripleGram, the
three-atom analogue of the landed design-level subsetSum_posDef_iff_tripleGram.

F55-3.  THE RIGHT TOOL: THE RANK-ONE DOWNDATE (commit 42f909f)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every triple of the trio is A_y LESS ONE OUTSIDE ATOM, and cell H carries
A_y > 0 as a hypothesis.  A rank-one downdate of a PosDef matrix moves at most
one eigenvalue across zero, so a positive determinant leaves none across.
Landed, GENERAL and reusable by every lane:
    Gtz.det_sub_vecMulVec        det(N - g g') = det N * (1 - g' N^-1 g)
                                 (matrix determinant lemma, via mathlib's
                                  Matrix.det_add_mul; NOT previously landed)
    Gtz.posDef_sub_vecMulVec_of_det_pos    N > 0 /\ det(N - g g') > 0
                                            =>  N - g g' > 0
    Gtz.posDef_sub_atomMatrix_of_det_pos   the atom form
    Gtz.cellH_exists_dominating_triple_of_posDef
        -- cell H yields a STRICTLY DOMINATING TRIPLE with NO admissibility

F55-5.  THE RESIDUAL, RESTATED -- AND IT IS NOT A DISJUNCTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
With F55-3 landed, cell H reduces to proving the trio fires.  Do NOT state that
as a disjunction and do NOT hunt a product-form probe for it: a sibling proved
the probe in prod(t - phi) <= 0 is irreducibly data-dependent (valid windows

which is a single Positivstellensatz target with no disjunction at all:

    { corner,  A_y > 0,  A_z NOT > 0,  T4 <= 0,  T5 <= 0,  T6 <= 0 }  =  EMPTY

where T4 = det(A_y - g4 g4'), etc.  This is simpler than the symmetric-function
form {e1<=0, e2>=0, e3<=0} that F48 recorded -- the three T_i <= 0 are directly
the negation and are equivalent to it.
