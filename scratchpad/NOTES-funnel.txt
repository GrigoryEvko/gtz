F1. THE SECOND-INVARIANT FLOOR, AND IT IS SHARP
-----------------------------------------------
The landed deflated gap bound (1-t_a)(S_T-1) - t_a(1-g_a g_a^T) >= 0 gives
   (1-t_a)^2 * e2(S_T-1) = e2(R) + t_a^2 + t_a*(tr R + g_a.R g_a)
with R the PSD slack. Every piece is nonnegative, so
   pairMinorTotal >= (t_a/(1-t_a))^2 > 0.
So a FUNNEL DOMINATOR IS NEVER A CORNER: its gap has corank exactly one.
This discharges the pairMinorTotal /= 0 hypothesis of the landed pair-minor
trigger (Gtz.unitAtom_parallel_of_two_pairMinors_zero_of_bound).

Four expansion identities verified to 7.1e-15 over 20,000 draws each:
   e2(V V^T - I) = pairMinorTotal(columns of V)      5.684e-14
   e2(X+Y) = e2 X + e2 Y + tr X tr Y - tr(XY)        7.105e-15
   e2(mu*(I-gg^T)) = mu^2 when |g|=1                 7.105e-15
   tr((I-gg^T)R) = tr R - g.Rg  (R SYMMETRIC ONLY)   3.553e-15
The last one is FALSE for non-symmetric R -- it reads the transpose. The Lean
statement carries the symmetry hypothesis.

SHARPNESS (slack R = 0 exactly):
   t=0.10  pairMinorTotal = 0.012345679   (t/(1-t))^2 = 0.012345679
   t=0.30                   0.183673469                 0.183673469
   t=0.50                   1.000000000                 1.000000000
   t=0.80                  16.000000000                16.000000000
Minimum ratio over 20,000 random deflated configurations: 1.000922.
No constant better than (t_a/(1-t_a))^2 exists.

F2. THREE BLIND ATOMS OVERSPEND THE PARSEVAL BUDGET  (new obstruction)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
At a (6,3) design with leverage(g_a) = 1, Parseval read at g_a gives
   t_a + sum_{c/=a} t_c (g_c.g_a)^2 = 1 .
If three of the five remaining atoms are blind to a, the two visible ones carry
the whole reading, t_x s_x^2 + t_y s_y^2 = 1 - t_a, while the funnel's own
resolution gives s_x^2 + s_y^2 = 1. Then
   1 - t_a = t_x s_x^2 + t_y s_y^2 <= t_x + t_y < 1 - t_a,
strict because the three blind atoms carry positive weight. CONTRADICTION.
A funnel has AT MOST TWO blind atoms, and the bound is on WEIGHTS, not geometry.

F3. A FUNNEL REFUSES A SECOND SWAP IN EVERY DIRECTION
-----------------------------------------------------
Second null-sharing swap of a funnel dominator T = {p,x,y}:
  (a) at a DIFFERENT member  -> landed Gtz.unitAtom_two_mirror_swaps_absurd
  (b) exchanging p for the unit atom a itself -> the mirror identity reads
      (g_p.g_a) g_p = (g_a.g_a) g_a = g_a, so g_a is a multiple of g_p: a
      PARALLEL PAIR outright  (Gtz.unitAtom_parallel_of_swap_to_atom)
  (c) exchanging p for the two remaining labels -> three blind atoms -> F2.
So at a design with no parallel pair a funnel dominator carries AT MOST ONE
null-sharing swap.

F4. THE COMBINATORIAL CAP -- AND A CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF
-----------------------------------------------------------
Triples of a 5-set <-> complementary edges of K5; "share two members" <-> the
edges are adjacent; the swap graph is the line graph of K5, degree six. Exact
brute force over all families:
   max family with swap-degree <= 1                         : 3   CONFIRMED
     example {0,1,2},{0,1,3},{2,3,4}
   max family with no triple having two neighbours at
     DIFFERENT members                                      : 4   <-- NOT 3
     example {0,1,2},{0,1,3},{0,1,4},{2,3,4}
The brief predicted 3 for the second reading. It is 4: three triples sharing one
common pair each have both neighbours at the SAME member. Because F3 closes the
same-member case too, the operative cap is the swap-degree one, i.e. 3.
Landed as Gtz.swapDegree_le_one_card_le_three by decision over 1024 families,
after re-encoding the family as Finset (Fin 10) -- quantifying over
Finset (Finset (Fin 5)) is 2^32 and does not decide.

F5. HOW MANY DOMINATORS DOES A (5,3) TIE HAVE?  (measured)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Ties located as exact zeros of  sum_T max(lambda_min(S_T - I), 0)^2  (the tie
locus is a MINIMUM locus, so plain minimisation of max lambda_min does not find
it -- 0/70 hits; this reformulation gets ~60% of starts).
71 ties found, objective < 1e-13. Dominator-count histogram:
   6 -> 1     7 -> 33     8 -> 37        MINIMUM SEEN: 6
The (5,3) diamond tie carries 8.
Against the cap of 3 from F3+F4, NO FUNNEL SURVIVES. The funnel branch of the
(6,3) dichotomy is therefore empty, CONDITIONAL on "every (5,3) tie has at least
four weakly dominating triples", which is measured (71/71, min 6) and NOT PROVED.
That conditional is the single remaining gap on this branch.

F6. THE FROZEN-WEDGE SEPARATOR IS REFUTED (with a witness), AND THE NOTION IS
    NOT WELL POSED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jacobian of their lambda_min against a 20- or 24-parameter whitened design
chart, take its null space as the tangent to the equality stratum, and ask which

       (3,4) 1.031e+00  (3,5) 6.195e-01  (4,5) 9.567e-01
"Exactly one frozen wedge, always the parallel pair" is FALSE under the
equality-stratum reading: the parallel pair's wedge moves at the same order as
every other pair.
And under an inequality reading the notion DEGENERATES: the tie locus is the
boundary of {all lambda_min <= 0}, its tangent cone is {A delta <= 0} which is
full dimensional, so a differential vanishing on it vanishes identically. There
is no reading in which the separator is a nontrivial true statement about this
fixture. DO NOT BUILD ON IT.
(The old harness reporting all 15 wedges frozen at exactly 0.0 was the
re-whitening artifact; the tell was that the output did not change after the
"fix". This harness never re-whitens a perturbed point.)
