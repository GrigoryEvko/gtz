P71-3  THE THREE-CLASS LAW.  This is the whole answer, and it is elementary.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let D be a rank-two tie, q_cd = pairGapForm D c d = det(S_{c,d} - 1), t_c the
weights, l_c the leverages.  Partition the atoms by PARALLELISM (pairBracket = 0,
an equivalence because a tie has no zero atom).  For a class X put
T_X = sum_{c in X} t_c, M_X = sum_{c in X} t_c l_c, and

    Q_XY = sum_{c in X} sum_{d in Y} t_c t_d q_cd .

Three landed inputs, no new analysis:
  (i)   HEAVINESS.  1 <= l_c at every atom.  A strictly light atom deflates to a
        STRICT dominator one size down (Gtz.posDef_of_strictly_light_atom fed by
        Gtz.gtz_rank_two), which a tie refuses.
  (ii)  SIGN LAW.  q_cd <= 0 off the diagonal.  A positive one at a heavy pivot
        is a strict dominator (Gtz.posDef_pairGap_of_pairGapForm_pos), and (i)
        supplies the pivot.
  (iii) MASTER IDENTITY.  sum_d t_d q_cd = -1 at rank two
        (Gtz.sum_weight_mul_pairGapForm_rankTwo).

Then, with CLASSDEFECT(X) = 1 + T_X - 2 M_X:

    sum_Y Q_XY = -T_X                          (iii), summed over X
    Q_XX       = -T_X (2 M_X - T_X)            Lagrange, vanishing bracket
    sum_{Y /= X} Q_XY = -T_X * CLASSDEFECT(X)  subtract

Cross terms are nonpositive by (ii) and T_X > 0, so CLASSDEFECT(X) >= 0.  But
Parseval gives sum_X T_X = 1 and sum_X M_X = 2, so the defects add to p - 3 where
p is the number of classes.  The four-direction hinge
(Gtz.not_isTie_of_fourNonParallel, landed and unconditional) gives p <= 3.
Hence p = 3, every CLASSDEFECT vanishes, every Q_XY with X /= Y vanishes, every
cross-class q_cd vanishes, and a vanishing gap determinant at two heavy atoms is
weak domination.  So:

    THE DOMINATING PAIRS OF A RANK-TWO TIE ARE EXACTLY ITS CROSS-CLASS PAIRS.

Note p >= 3 falls out of the SAME budget -- no separate argument for one or two
classes is needed.  Note also that the corank-one identity
Gtz.leverage_identity_of_isTie_rankTwoCorankOne is the case m = 3 of the budget
(p - 3 = 0 forces every defect to vanish), so the class algebra is that identity
transported to the classes of a larger tie without ever building the merged
three-atom design.

P71-6  DEAD ENDS, so nobody repeats them
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The spectral route to the class structure -- F = P.P - delta delta^T, S =
  diag(t)^-1 F stochastic with trace 2k-1, rank <= 3 forcing S idempotent -- is
  CORRECT and was fully worked out, including an eigenvalue-free proof (D_t - F
  is a graph Laplacian, hence PSD, hence every eigenvalue of S is at most one;
  at most three are positive because F is a rank-one downdate of a rank-three
  Gram; trace three then pins the spectrum at (1,1,1,0,0)).  It needs orthogonal
  projections, traces of subspaces and a rank-nullity extraction.  The class
  algebra above reaches the SAME conclusion with three landed identities and no
  linear algebra at all.  Do not build the spectral route.
* Merging the classes into a three-atom design and quoting the corank-one
  identity also works (the merge IS a design, and T_X T_Y qhat_XY = Q_XY
  exactly), but constructing the merged design in Lean costs more than the class
  algebra, which is the same computation done in place.
* "Every (5,2) design has at least four dominating pairs" is FALSE without the
  tie: two heavy orthogonal atoms plus three tiny ones give exactly one.
