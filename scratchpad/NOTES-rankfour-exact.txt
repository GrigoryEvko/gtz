F-R4X-6.  WHAT IS STILL OPEN, AND THE NEXT TARGET.
---------------------------------------------------
Open: a parallel-free tie at (8,4) is not refuted, only unreached over 800
restarts with an inactive constraint.  The provable form of the target is

  B(eps) > 0 for every eps > 0, at m = 2*rank,

where B(eps) is the constrained infimum of max_C lmin(M[C,C]) over designs with
cos^2 <= 1 - eps.  Note B(eps) -> 0, so no absolute constant exists; only the
positivity at each eps is true, and that is exactly the hinge.

THE STRUCTURAL REASON TO EXPECT 2*rank, and the next thing to land.  At uniform
weights the cell m = 2*rank is SELF-DUAL under Gale duality: with S_univ = m*I,
a k-subset C dominates in D exactly when its complement dominates in the Naimark
dual, which has rank m - k.  For m < 2k the dual has rank BELOW k, so the
question descends; at m = 2k the dual has the same rank and no descent exists.
The campaign carries this only at (6,3)
(Gtz.dominates_iff_dominates_chartDual_compl_of_uniformWeight_sixThree,
Gtz.isTie_chartDual_iff_isTie_of_uniformWeight, and
Gtz.selfDual_ne_top_of_ne_three which notes the threshold cell is self-dual only
at rank three).  What is NOT noticed anywhere: the SELF-DUAL cell is m = 2*rank
at every rank, and that is precisely the band's lower endpoint.  The general-rank
statement is within reach, because the two Sylvester comparison lemmas it needs
are already general — Gtz.posSemidef_transpose_mul_sub_one_comm and
Gtz.posSemidef_smul_one_sub_transpose_comm — and the Naimark dual design is
already constructed at general rank in Gtz/Wave/NaimarkDualDesign.lean.
