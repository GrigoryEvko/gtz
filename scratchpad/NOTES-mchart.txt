THE QUADRATIC PRESENTATION -- what is actually new

  M M + M T + T M + T T = M + T      (T = diag t)

(`Gtz.chartQuadratic_iff_mul_self`). So a design is a pair (M, t) with

  M symmetric, t > 0, sum t = 1, the equation above, and tr M = rank - 1.

`Gtz.ChartCore` is those five items and `Gtz.exists_design_of_chartCore` sends
one back to a design with the SAME weights and the SAME gap. Hence

  Gtz.hingeHoldsAtSize_iff_chartCore     -- at EVERY (m,k)

THE COMPLEMENTARY BLOCK LAW -- why ONE block carries BOTH shifts

  det P[C,C] = det (1 - P)[C^c,C^c]     for |C| = rank

and it is NOT Jacobi's complementary minor identity. Both sides are det(V_C)^2:
the left is det(V_C V_C^T); the right is det(1 - V_{C^c} V_{C^c}^T), which the
Weinstein-Aronszajn flip (Matrix.det_one_add_mul_comm, already used in the tree)
turns into det(1 - V_{C^c}^T V_{C^c}), and V^T V = 1 splits as
V_C^T V_C + V_{C^c}^T V_{C^c} = 1. Four lines. Mathlib does NOT have Jacobi and
this route does not need it.

In the gap that reads

  det(M[C,C] + T_C) = det(1 - M[C^c,C^c] - T_{C^c})

-- the FIRST shift on a block is the SECOND shift on the COMPLEMENTARY block.
That is why one 2x2 block of M carries both halves of the pair dictionary, and at
(6,3) it pairs the twenty 3x3 blocks into TEN complementary couples. Design-free
form: Gtz.chartCore_det_gapBlock_shift_eq.

It is an equality of DETERMINANTS, not of definiteness. At 3x3 a positive
determinant survives two negative eigenvalues, which is exactly the parity failure
Gtz.tripleGapDet_nonpos_iff_naimark already records. It constrains a (6,3) tie and
decides nothing. Do not expect it to close the cell.
