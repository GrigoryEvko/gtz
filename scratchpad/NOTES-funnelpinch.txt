1. WHAT IS NEW

The gap of a funnel dominator is positive semidefinite AND singular, so its
adjugate is rank one:

    adj G = e2 * w wT                      (adjugate_eq_smul_atomMatrix_of_unit_null)

The corpus carried only the QUADRATIC FORM of this (adjugate_reading_of_unit_null).
Polarization (eq_of_quadForm_eq) upgrades it to a matrix identity, and with the
hypothesis-free adjugate polynomial adj M = M^2 - (tr M) M + e2 M * 1 it gives

    G * G = tau * G - e2 * (1 - w wT)      (mul_self_of_unit_null)

which is two-by-two Cayley-Hamilton on the plane w-perp, carried on a
three-by-three matrix.  No eigenvalue, no square root, no spectral theorem.

Consequences landed:
  * (G + w wT)^-1 = (tau*1 - G + (e2 - tau)*w wT)/e2      (inv_kernelShift_eq)
    The whole KernelSlideDropLaw layer carried `IsUnit (kernelShift gap kern).det`
    as a hypothesis.  It is now a THEOREM from e2 != 0 (isUnit_det_kernelShift).
  * trace (G + w wT)^-1 = 1 + tau/e2                      (trace_inv_kernelShift)
  * N * G = 1 - w wT                                      (kernelShiftInverse_mul_self)
  * sum over a triple of the shifted forms = 3 + tau/e2   (triple_inverseForm_sum_of_unit_null)
  * 4*e2 <= tau^2                                         (four_mul_secondInvariant_le_trace_sq_of_unit_null)

2. THE TWO ROUTES ARE ONE LAW  (readingCap_sub_eq_payment_sub, readingCap_iff_payment)

  e2 * (cap deficit) = (payment deficit),  exactly.

So Gtz.reading_cap_of_isTie (three drop refusals, KernelSlideDropLaw) and
Gtz.isTie_funnel_payment (four-set determinant, FunnelFourSetPayment) carry
IDENTICAL information.  Do not spend effort on both.  Measured: max discrepancy
2.2e-11 over 300000 draws.

Because the cap route needs no unit atom, the payment law GENERALIZES off the
funnel: payment_law_of_isTie holds at every corank-one weak dominator of every
boundary system, at any size m.

3. THE PINCH, AND THE ELIMINATION

For h orthogonal to the kernel, with n = h.h and s = h.(G h):

    ||G h||^2 = tau*s - e2*n     and    s^2 <= n*||G h||^2
  =>  s^2 - tau*n*s + e2*n^2 <= 0                      (pinch_of_kernelOrth)

One polynomial inequality carrying the whole statement "s/n lies between the two
nonzero eigenvalues".  At an atom v the slid probe h = v - (v.w) w has
n = leverage - reading^2 and the SAME gap reading as v (atom_pinch).

Write M = leverage*tau - e2*(reading^2 - 1), the payment ceiling.  The producer
fires exactly when s > M.  The brief's two eliminants are then not new
polynomials:

    K = tau*n - 2*M          Q = M^2 - tau*n*M + e2*n^2 = pinchPoly at M.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF.  The brief's eigenvalue shape "e2*(r^2-1) > lam2*l +
lam1*r^2" has lam1 and lam2 interchanged.  The correct firing condition is

    leverage*lam1 + reading^2*lam2 < e2*(reading^2 - 1) ,

with lam1 the LARGER root multiplying the leverage.  Equivalently

    n*lam1 < e2*(reading^2 - 1) - reading^2*tau .

4. WHAT THE WINDOW NEEDS  (trace_lt_secondInvariant_of_window, one_lt_reading_of_window)

An open window forces

    tau < e2      and      e2 < reading^2 * (e2 - tau) ,   hence reading^2 > 1 .

Reading tau/e2 = 1/lam1 + 1/lam2, the first says 1/lam1 + 1/lam2 < 1.  The
second pins the window to atoms whose squared reading exceeds one, which is
exactly where Gtz.exists_outside_reading_sq_gt_one puts an atom of a funnel.
Measured: 1479 open windows in 40000 draws, zero violations of the three
conditions.

5. WHERE IT STOPS: THE PLANE NORM OF THE OUTSIDE READER

The window fires at atom p exactly when

    n_p * lam1 < e2*(R_p - 1) - R_p*tau ,     n_p = leverage_p - R_p .

Everything on the right is a quantity of the DOMINATOR plus the reading.  Nothing
in the landed floors bounds n_p from ABOVE.  The only available bound is
Parseval's t_p * leverage_p <= 1, so n_p <= 1/t_p, which DIVERGES as the reader's
weight goes to zero.  A successor must bound the outside reader's squared
component ORTHOGONAL to the unit atom by a quantity of the dominator.

6. MEASUREMENTS  (scratchpad/funnelpinch/)

Construction: exact rank-two closure.  g0 = e3 the unit atom; T = {1,2,3} with
plane parts p1,p2,p3 free and the z-components the unit kernel vector of
[p1 p2 p3], so S_T e3 = e3 by construction; t0 = det B / cof33(B) makes the
residual A = I - t0 e3e3T - sum_T t_c g_c g_cT singular; A closes on the two
outside atoms.  Every design built is an exact (6,3) funnel: sum t = 1,
leverage_a = 1, S_T g_a = g_a, gap PSD.

  * 251581 funnel designs.  The window fires at 35.5 percent.  74.9 percent have
    e2 >= tau, so the first window condition alone is not the obstruction.
