1. JOB B ANSWERED: THE RANK-TWO COINCIDENCE IS SPECIAL TO RANK TWO
------------------------------------------------------------------
The rank-k coupling is pinned by three normalizations, not chosen:

  F_cd = t_c t_d (1 - k(k-1) + (k-1)(l_c + l_d) - l_c l_d + P_cd^2)

Parseval supplies only three sums (Sum t_d = 1, Sum t_d l_d = k,
Sum t_d P_cd^2 = l_c), so a row sum exists only for the four-parameter family
a + b(l_c+l_d) + e l_c l_d + c P_cd^2. The row law forces a + bk = 1 and
b + ek + c = 0. A design-independent trace forces e + c = 0. The Veronese scale
c = 1 closes it. At k = 2 the result IS the shipped Gtz.tieCoupling, proved as
Gtz.veroneseCoupling_eq_tieCoupling.

  ROW LAW    Sum_d F_cd = t_c            at every rank, no hypothesis
  TRACE LAW  Sum_c F_cc / t_c = k^2-k+1  at every rank, no hypothesis

The trace constant is 3 at rank two (the shipped 2k-1) and SEVEN at rank three.
Nonnegative entries cap each term of the trace law at one, so k^2-k+1 <= m.

  (6,3)  7 <= 6 is false.  Gtz.exists_veroneseCoupling_neg_sixThree
  (7,3)  a Parseval dependency is a null direction of the coupling, and one null
         direction buys one unit of trace, so 7 <= 7-1 is false.
         Gtz.exists_veroneseCoupling_neg_sevenThree

So entrywise nonnegativity -- the direct transport of the tie hypothesis through
the Laplacian -- is not merely unproved at rank three. It is FALSE at both cells.

The arithmetic that explains it: the Veronese image lives in Sym_k(R), of
dimension k(k+1)/2, and the trace law asks for k^2-k+1. The two agree exactly
when k^2 + 2 <= 3k, that is when k <= 2.
