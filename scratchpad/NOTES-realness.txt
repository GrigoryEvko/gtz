6. WHAT IS LEFT, AND WHERE TO SPEND THE REALNESS
------------------------------------------------
The realness budget of a corank-two corner sits on the three four-sets {e} + C^c
and on the five-sets that carry the whole dominator. Concretely, and measured at
the complex witness (scratchpad/heroncorner.py):

  (1+lam)*|[x p q]|^2 = |A - B|^2 ,  A = [xyp][xzq] ,  B = [xyq][xzp] .

That equality holds over BOTH fields, because the three-term relation is
field-blind. Over R the right side is (|A| - |B|)^2 or (|A| + |B|)^2, a TWO-POINT
set. Over C it is any value in the closed interval between them. At the complex
witness every one of the nine instances sits STRICTLY INSIDE:

  x=2, {3,4}:   20.16   inside [0.85, 53.23]
  x=2, {3,5}:    2.53   inside [0.60,  8.77]
  x=0, {3,4}:   50.69   inside [16.26, 52.54]

So the informative bracket of a real corner is pinned to two values by corner
data, and the corner has nine such instances plus the outside triple. THAT is the
system a closing certificate must contradict. It is quartic, it consumes the
field at a named step, and no landed law reaches it.
