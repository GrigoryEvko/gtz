2. THE Z-PICTURE (the coordinate that made the census visible)
---------------------------------------------------------------
W := S_univ − 1 ≻ 0.  Put z_a := W^{-1/2} g_a.  Then P_ab = z_a·z_b, the
Parseval identity becomes Σ_a (1−t_a) z_a z_aᵀ = I, and Σ_a (1−t_a) = 5.  For
every subset S,  S dominates strictly ⟺ Σ_{a∈S} z_a z_aᵀ ≻ W^{-1}, and for a
triple R,  Rᶜ dominates strictly ⟺ Σ_{r∈R} z_r z_rᵀ ≺ I.  A corank-two corner
reads Σ_{d∉C} z_d z_dᵀ = I − π p pᵀ with π = cornerPivot ∈ (0,1] and
1 − P_dd = π(p·z_d)²/(1−π).  Not mechanized: it is a change of coordinates, not
new mathematics.

3. THE CENSUS (landed)
-----------------------
(S_T − 1) + g_e g_eᵀ = lam·u uᵀ + g_d g_dᵀ for T = (C \ {e}) ∪ {d}.  The right
side is a sum of two rank-one atoms in three dimensions, hence singular, and its
kernel direction reads S_T − 1 at −(g_e·z)² ≤ 0.  So every triple sharing at
least two atoms with C fails to dominate strictly, with NO tie hypothesis: ten
of the twenty.  A strict dominator therefore shares at most one atom with C, and
the tie condition at a corner is exactly the ten remaining refusals — the
complement Cᶜ and the nine one-inside triples {e,d',d''}.

4. THE FOUR SLACKS OF A PAIR, AND THE SCHUR-HORN CAP
------------------------------------------------------
For an admissible inside pair {f,h}, N := z_f z_fᵀ + z_h z_hᵀ ≺ I and
R := (I−N)^{-1}.  The four landed slacks are ONE statement:
  triple {f,h,a} refuses  ⟺  z_aᵀ R z_a ≥ 1 .
Aggregating with the co-weights gives `pairRefusalLedger`; aggregating over the
complement gives `corner_pairRefusal_bound`, i.e. tr(R(I − π p pᵀ)) ≥ 3, i.e.
  (*)  a + b ≥ (2+π)Δ + π(b α² + 2c αβ + a β²) ,  α = p·z_f, β = p·z_h .

MEASURED (416 + 522 exactly-Parseval corners, float, sampler in corner_probe.py):
* every corner carries a strict dominator (0 exceptions), so the corner is empty
* every strict dominator shares at most one atom with C (0 exceptions)
* (*) fires at only 116 of 522 corners, while the INDIVIDUAL refusals
  z_dᵀRz_d < 1 fire at all 468 corners where a one-inside triple dominates.
So the individual slacks do carry more than their sum, as the brief says.

BUT the extra content is NOT in one pair.  Writing w_d := (P_fd, P_hd, γ_d) the
three outside refusals read w_dᵀ J w_d ≥ 0 with J = diag(adj K₂, −πΔ/(1−π)), and
Σ_d w_d w_dᵀ is a FIXED matrix G₃ determined by (a,b,c,α,β,π).  So the three
refusals are o_dᵀ H o_d ≥ 0 over an orthonormal basis {o_d}, H := G₃^{1/2} J
G₃^{1/2}, and by Schur–Horn such a basis exists exactly when tr H ≥ 0, which is
(*).  For one pair with the outside frame free, the individual refusals and
their sum are EQUIVALENT.  The gain is entirely in the coupling: the same outside
frame serves all three inside pairs.  Any programme that works pair by pair is
capped at (*).

5. THE SYMMETRIC SLICE — WHERE THE WEIGHTS ENTER
-------------------------------------------------
π = 1, p = e₃, outside = Mercedes frame in p^⊥ with weight σ each, inside =
Mercedes cone (r,w) with weight ς each.  Parseval gives w² = 1/(3ς),
r² = 2(1−σ)/(3ς); Σ(1−t) = 5 gives σ + ς = 5/3.  Then the violation of (*) is

    2(3ς − 2)(1 − ς) / (9ς²)  >  0   for ς ∈ (2/3, 1) ,

vanishing exactly at ς → 1 (inside weight → 0) and ς → 2/3 (outside weight → 0).
Without Σ t = 1 the violation goes negative (1/ς = 3, small r gives −0.99).  So
the certificate needs BOTH t_a > 0 and Σ t_a = 1, and the second is where the
size six enters.  `corner_gapScale_floor_of_isTie` has exactly that shape.

6. THE GAP SCALE WINDOW (landed)
---------------------------------
Ceiling, tie-free:  weightFloor·(1 + lam) ≤ 1.
Floor, at a tie:    1 ≤ ceilIn·(1 + lam) + ceilOut,
so 1 − t_e − t_d ≤ t_e·lam for a heaviest inside e and a heaviest outside d, and
`weight_pair_lt_one` makes the left side strictly positive.  The floor consumes
`htie.2 Cᶜ` with Cᶜ.card = 3, so it is VACUOUS at (5,3), where the complement of
a triple is a pair.  That is the (5,3) break.

7. WHAT IS LEFT, AND THE ONE HARD STEP
----------------------------------------
Write A := 1 − Z_C in the z-picture (equivalently Λ := S_{Cᶜ} − 1 in design
coordinates).  A tie at a corner says
  (i)  Λ ⊁ 0 ,
  (ii) Λ + g_e g_eᵀ − g_d g_dᵀ ⊁ 0  for every e ∈ C and d ∈ Cᶜ .
The plane cover makes Λ ≻ 0 on u^⊥, so Λ fails in exactly one direction, and the
quantitative cover pins that direction to the axis.  MEASURED on 253 corners with
λ_max(Z_C) ≥ 1 (the branch a tie must live in, corner_probe3.py):
  * an admissible inside pair always exists (0 exceptions)
  * e := argmax_e (z_e·φ)², φ the top eigenvector of Z_C, is always admissible
    AND always completes to a strict dominator (253/253)
  * no single rule for d works: argmin_d ‖z_d‖² succeeds 236/253.
So the missing step is: prove that the argmax-φ inside atom yields an admissible
pair, and that some outside atom then completes.  The second half is where the
count nine-versus-three separates (6,3) from (5,3).
