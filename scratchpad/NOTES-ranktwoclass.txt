1. THE THEOREM
--------------

Let D : WeightedDesign m 2 be an exact tie.  Then:

  * the atoms fall into exactly THREE nonempty classes
    (Gtz.card_image_tieClass_eq_three);
  * two atoms share a class if and only if they are PARALLEL
    (Gtz.mem_tieClass_iff_planeWedge_eq_zero);
  * on a class the leverage is constant and the class weight dictates it,
    (2 l_c - 1) T_A = 1, that is l_c = (1 + 1/T_A)/2
    (Gtz.two_leverage_sub_one_mul_classWeight);
  * every cross-class pair weakly dominates
    (Gtz.dominates_of_planeWedge_ne_zero);
  * every within-class pair has gap determinant -1/T_A
    (Gtz.det_pairGap_mul_classWeight_of_mem_tieClass).

The capstone that bundles the five fields is Gtz.rankTwoTieClassification.

2. THE MECHANISM, AND THE ONE IDENTITY THAT MAKES IT CHEAP
----------------------------------------------------------

The pair gap determinant at rank two is

    det(S_{c,d} - 1) = J_cd^2 - (l_c + l_d - 1),      J_cd = the wedge,

so the whole domination question for a pair is the sign of one scalar.  Define

    F_cd := t_c t_d (l_c + l_d - 1 - J_cd^2).

Three facts drive the file.

  (a) THE ROW LAW  sum_d F_cd = t_c, at EVERY rank-two design, no hypothesis.
      It is the wedge row law sum_d t_d J_cd^2 = l_c, which is Lagrange fed by the
      tree's two Parseval readings (sum_weight_mul_sq_atomPairing and
      sum_weighted_leverage).
  (b) THE LAPLACIAN  x.(diag t - F)x = (1/2) sum F_cd (x_c - x_d)^2.  Entrywise
      nonnegativity plus the row law give a nonnegative form with no spectral theorem.
  (c) THE VERONESE FACTORISATION  N = R R^T - s s^T with R of THREE columns, because
      Sym^2(R^2) is three-dimensional.  The trace of N is 2k - 1 = 3.

The projection theorem (Gtz.normalizedCoupling_eq_couplingProjection) is a TRACE
SANDWICH, not an eigenvalue count: 1 - N has a nonnegative form and trace m - 3, the
projection Pi onto ker R^T has trace m - 3, and 1 - N dominates the identity on the
range of Pi, so tr(Pi (1-N) Pi - Pi) and tr((1-Pi)(1-N)(1-Pi)) are two nonnegative
traces summing to zero.  A nonnegative form of zero trace is zero, and the two
equations compose to 1 - N = Pi.  This is Gtz.eq_of_trace_sandwich and it is reusable.

Then N is an orthogonal projection with nonnegative entries and a positive diagonal,
so the class relation is an equivalence whose classes are cliques, and ONE Perron step
(Gtz.tieCoupling_mul_classWeight) pins each block to F_cd T_A = t_c t_d.  The Perron
step is short because the column of a projection is ALREADY nonnegative: subtract the
smallest multiple of the square-root weight vector, and the row of the fixed-point
equation at the minimiser kills the residue.

The DIAGONAL of the block formula reads 2 l_c - 1 = 1/T_A; its OFF-DIAGONAL then reads
J_cd = 0.  Same class, parallel, equal leverage: one line each.  The class count is the
trace, 3 = sum_c t_c/T_c.

3. THE FREE COROLLARY
---------------------

Gtz.weight_mul_two_leverage_sub_one_le_one:  t_d (2 l_d - 1) <= 1, equivalently
l_d <= (1 + t_d)/(2 t_d).  Three lines from (a) and entrywise nonnegativity ONLY.  It
needs no tie, no dominating pair, only heaviness and no strictly dominating pair.  The
tree did not carry it in this form.

4. WHAT DOES NOT LIFT TO RANK THREE, MEASURED
---------------------------------------------

At rank k the matrix with row sums t is P o P - (1/(k-1)) d d^T.  At the (5,3) diamond
(t = 1/5, share 0.65) its DIAGONAL already exceeds its row sum, so it is not entrywise
nonnegative and the Laplacian step (b) dies.  Three ingredients are rank-two only:
pairs ARE the selections, rank(P o P) <= dim Sym^2 R^k = 3 only at k = 2, and
tr S = 2k - 1 = 3 only at k = 2.  No attempt was made to lift.

5. THE RANK-THREE PAYLOAD
--------------------------

Every two-dimensional subspace of R^3 carries a rank-two shadow of a rank-three design
(Gtz.inPlaneRestriction), so the classification reads on the whole sphere of normals.

  Gtz.planeShadow_three_classes -- the shadow of a rank-three design whose shadow is
  heavy and carries no strictly dominating pair has exactly three classes, and the
  classes are the classes of the FRAME BRACKET
  (g_c . p1)(g_d . p2) - (g_c . p2)(g_d . p1), which the tree reads as the triple
  bracket against the normal
  (Gtz.pairBracket_inPlaneRestriction_eq_frameOrientation_mul_tripleBracket).

  Gtz.planeShadow_exists_frameBracket_eq_zero -- at four or more atoms such a plane
  carries two atoms whose shadows are parallel.

7. OPEN, AND DELIBERATELY NOT CLAIMED
--------------------------------------

* Nothing about rank three beyond the plane-shadow reading.  The shadow hypotheses
  (shadow heaviness, no plane-strict pair) are REAL hypotheses and are not discharged
  here.
* The relation to Gtz.not_isTie_of_fourNonParallel: that theorem already reaches the
  rank-two hinge by the Bloch-chart equality stratum.  Gtz.hingeHoldsAtSize_rank_two
  is NOT a reproof of it -- it is a two-line consequence of the classification, which
  is strictly more information.  Neither file is deleted and neither supersedes the
  other.
* No claim that the weights of a rank-two tie are uniform.  At three atoms
  Gtz.rankTwoTie_three_atoms gives t_c (2 l_c - 1) = 1 for every atom, which the tie
  at t = (0.0102, 0.9785, 0.0113) satisfies.  There is no Mercedes rigidity.
