STEP 1 -- THE COLLAPSE.  LANDED.
--------------------------------
Fact 1 was ALREADY IN THE TREE as Gtz.leverage_eq_one_of_nullReadings_zero
(Gtz/Wave/CorankOneGramMirror.lean).  Fact 2 likewise, as the dot-product form
Gtz.zeroAxis_leverage_eq_one.  Neither is new.  What is new is that
Gtz.hasParallelPair_of_isTie_sixThree_of_unitAtom landed this session, so both
compose:

  Gtz.hasParallelPair_of_isTie_sixThree_of_twoZeroReadings   -- K2 collapses
  Gtz.hasParallelPair_of_isTie_sixThree_of_zeroAxisReading   -- Z1 collapses

Cells H and B and both horns live at a Z1 corner, so all four are discharged as
hinge residuals without proving any of them.

THE ENGINE is one hypothesis-free polynomial identity,
Gtz.leverageSub_mul_tripleGapDet_add_sq:

  (l_a - 1) * tripleGapDet a b c + ((l_a-1)(b.c) - (a.b)(a.c))^2
      = pairGapMinor a b * pairGapMinor a c

the Sylvester-Jacobi relation of the 3x3 gap read at the atom a.  Three readings:
 * q_ab = 0 and l_a > 1  =>  tripleGapDet a b c <= 0 for EVERY c, no design needed
 * two vanishing minors at a dominator force the third
 * a corank-two corner vanishes all three, since (1+lam)^2 q_ef
   = (lam w_e^2)(lam w_f^2) - (lam w_e w_f)^2 = 0

TRICHOTOMY (Gtz.tripleStratum_trichotomy): a dominating triple of an all-heavy
design has 0, 1 or 3 vanishing inside pair minors, NEVER exactly 2.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF.  The brief expected the residual to be "corner-Z0 or K0".
It is corner, K1 AND K0.  K1 does NOT collapse: one vanishing pair minor gives no
unit atom.  The dictionary is Gtz.pairGapMinor_eq_zero_of_one_nullReading_zero --
one zero null reading forces the OPPOSITE pair minor to vanish, because the
dominator relation gives <g_x,w>(l_x-1) = -<g_y,w> p_xy and its mirror, and
multiplying cancels the two nonzero readings.  So K2 is the ONLY stratum removed.

STEP 2 -- THE CORNER.  DOES NOT FALL.  Reported as a near miss, not a kill.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NUMERICS (calibrated: split diamond F = 1.4e-15, Parseval 6.7e-16, all-heavy).

(a) CENSUS.  Every dominating triple of every rank-three tie the campaign owns has
    corank exactly ONE.  (5,3) diamond: 8 dominating triples, 0 of corank 2.  All
    five (6,3) splits of it: 12 or 13 dominating triples each, 0 of corank 2.
    (4,3) tetrahedron: 4 triples, 0 of corank 2.  Not one corner anywhere.

(b) EXACT 15-PARAMETER CORNER SEARCH.  Parametrise the corner exactly, so that
    S_C - 1 = lam u u^T AND Parseval both hold to 4e-16 by construction:
      inside triple  = (1 + (sqrt(1+lam)-1) u u^T) O,  O orthogonal
      outside triple = R^{1/2} Q / sqrt(t_d),  R = 1 - sum_{c in C} t_c g_c g_c^T
    Minimising F = max_T lambda_min(S_T - 1) over the 15 parameters stalls at
      F = +4.34e-3  with parallel pairs allowed
      F = +1.00e-2  with cos^2 <= 0.98 enforced
    three orders above a tie, and it drives the inside leverages toward ONE, which
    is the funnel boundary this module collapses.  So the corner stratum LOOKS
    empty at (6,3).  It is NOT proved empty.

TWO NO-GOS, both proved, both worth banking so no lane repeats them.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NO-GO 1, THE MOMENT ROUTE IS VACUOUS AT A CORNER.  Landed as
Gtz.sum_outside_weight_mul_tripleGapDet_corner.  Read the third Sylvester moment
sum_c t_c tripleGapDet(a,b,c) = 2 - l_a - l_b at an inside pair of a corner.  The
pair minor vanishes, so crossNormSq = l_e + l_f - 1, the diagonal terms are
1 - l_f and 1 - l_e, and the third inside atom contributes 0.  What is left is

  sum_{d outside} t_d tripleGapDet(g_e,g_f,g_d) = -(l_e-1)(1-t_f) - (l_f-1)(1-t_e)

The right side is strictly negative and every summand on the left is nonpositive by
the cap, so the two sides agree in sign and the relation carries NO information.  It
is worse than vacuous: by hand it is an IDENTITY.  Put y_ef := <g_e,u> g_f -
<g_f,u> g_e.  Then y_ef is orthogonal to u, and
  <g_e,y_ef> = -<g_f,u>,  <g_f,y_ef> = <g_e,u>,  <g_g,y_ef> = 0,
  |y_ef|^2 = <g_e,u>^2 + <g_f,u>^2,
and -tripleGapDet(g_e,g_f,g_d) = (lam/(1+lam)) <g_d,y_ef>^2.  Parseval on y_ef then
reproduces the right side exactly.  DO NOT spend a cycle on a moment argument at a
corner.

NO-GO 2, THE AREA WINDOW NEVER CLOSES.  The landed ceiling
Gtz.crossNormSq_le_of_isTie against the admissibility floor reduces, with
X = l_p - 1, Y = l_q - 1, to  t_p Y + t_q X < X + Y, i.e. X(1-t_q) + Y(1-t_p) > 0,
which two weights below one always satisfy.  Confirms the parent's reading.

WHAT A SUCCESSOR SHOULD USE
---------------------------
The corner is completely pinned, and two theorems say so:
  Gtz.corner_planeProbe_tight  -- for every y orthogonal to u,
      sum_{c in C} <g_c,y>^2 = |y|^2 -- an UNWEIGHTED tight frame on the plane
  Gtz.corner_axisProbe_total   -- sum_{c in C} <g_c,u>^2 = 1 + lam
Together: the inside triple is an orthonormal basis stretched along the axis by
sqrt(1+lam).  Its plane Gram is I_3 - v v^T with |v| = 1 and v_c = <g_c,u>/sqrt(1+lam),
and all-heavy is exactly every v_c nonzero (Gtz.corner_axisReading_ne_zero_of_allHeavy).

The refusal count at a corner drops to TEN of twenty for free: the corner itself is
corank two, and all nine two-inside-one-outside triples fail because their inside
pair minor vanishes.  Only the complement triple and the nine one-inside-two-outside
triples carry the tie.

One necessary condition I derived by hand but did NOT land, because it degenerates at
a simplex vertex and so cannot be strict:  1 <= (max outside weight) +
(1+lam)(max inside weight).  It is the universal obstruction 1 - sum t_c^2 again.
