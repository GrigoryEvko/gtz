Round notes.  Every number below was produced by a harness calibrated

F75B-1.  THE STATEMENT REDUCES TO SIZE FIVE.  *** STRUCTURAL ***
  Splitting one atom of a boundary system into two collinear copies with
  half its weight each gives a boundary system one size larger:
    - Parseval is preserved (same vectors, weights t_a/2 + t_a/2 = t_a);
    - a triple holding BOTH copies has rank <= 2, so it cannot dominate;
    - a triple holding one copy has exactly the smaller system's S_C,
      so it dominates iff that one did, weakly and never strictly.
  All leverages, pairings, pair minors and wedges of the original are
  unchanged.  Hence an inadmissible NON-collinear pair at (5,3) lifts to
  one at (6,3):
      *** 7.5(b) at (6,3)  =>  7.5(b) at (5,3). ***
  So the size-five case is a PREREQUISITE, not a corollary, and it is the
  cheaper place to attack or refute: 20 parameters, 10 triples, and
  m = k+2 puts it inside the corank floor where rank two is available.
  VERIFIED exactly: split diamond, Parseval residual 6.7e-16,
  max_T lmin = 1.3e-15, 12 of 20 triples dominate, the only inadmissible
  pair is the collinear one (q = -3.0, w = 0.0).
  Gtz.splitDesign (Gtz/Reduction/SplitTransfer.lean:417) is this
  construction, already landed; the IsTie transfer is not.

F75B-2.  THE PAIR RUNG IS AN EIGENVALUE STATEMENT, AND IT SOLVES.
  Gtz.not_posDef_pairCap_of_isTie says (t_a+t_b)*1 - P_ab is not PosDef,
  i.e.  lmax(P_ab) >= t_a + t_b,  where P_ab = t_a g_a g_a^T + t_b g_b g_b^T.
  P_ab has the same nonzero spectrum as the 2x2 Gram [[s_a,p],[p,s_b]] with
  s_c = t_c l_c and p = sqrt(t_a t_b) <g_a,g_b>, so
      tr = S = s_a + s_b,     det = X = t_a t_b w_ab
  (the WEDGE is the determinant of the 2x2 Gram), and
      lmax = (S + sqrt(S^2 - 4X))/2 .
  Hence exactly:      *** lmax >= T  <=>  S >= 2T  or  X <= T(S-T) ***
  with T = t_a + t_b, and S - T = alpha_a + alpha_b the weighted excess.
  VERIFIED: 0 mismatches / 200,000 random pairs.
  The landed three-clause form Gtz.pairCap_scalar_of_isTie is equivalent
  (Descartes: for symmetric 3x3, PosDef <=> e1,e2,e3 > 0; verified 0
  mismatches / 100,000).  LANDED as Gtz.pairCap_solved_of_isTie.

F75B-3.  WHY THE CAP CANNOT REACH THE TARGET, IN ONE LINE.
  The second clause reads  w_ab <= T(alpha_a + alpha_b)/(t_a t_b).
  Its right side vanishes only when BOTH leverages are exactly one, so the
  cap can force w_ab = 0 only in that degenerate case.  This is exact, not
  a failure of effort: the cap is the sharp consequence of the rung.
  Two true readings survive and are landed:
    - at a collinear pair the bound holds with nothing spent (w = 0 and
      heaviness makes the right side >= 0) -- the rung is an identity
      there because P_ab has rank one and lmax = tr
      (Gtz.pairCap_vacuous_of_collinear);
    - at a non-collinear pair in the regime S < 2T the bound FORCES
      alpha_a + alpha_b > 0, so one member is strictly heavy
      (Gtz.exists_strictly_heavy_of_crossNormSq_pos).
  MEASURED: over six exact boundary systems (split diamond, split
  tetrahedron, four found as zeros of sum_T max(lmin,0)^2 under a weight
  floor 0.05) the rung has worst slack +9.5e-2 over 90 pairs, and the
  wedge bound worst slack +5.9e-2 over the 7 pairs in its regime.  The cap
  never binds on any of them.

F75B-4.  THE WEIGHT-RESCALED RUNG.  *** SHARPER THAN THE LADDER ***
  For any triple T and any tau >= max_{x in T} t_x,
      tau * S_T - P_T = sum_{x in T} (tau - t_x) g_x g_x^T  >=  0 ,
  so tau * S_T >= P_T.  Hence  lmin(P_T) > max_{x in T} t_x  =>  S_T > I.
  Contrapositive at a boundary system:
      *** lmin(P_T) <= max_{x in T} t_x  at every triple ***
  which is strictly sharper than the ladder's lmin(P_T) <= t_x+t_y+t_z, and
  needs NO erasure, NO corank floor and NO smaller instance of GTZ.
  Dual form via P_T + P_{T^c} = I:  lmax(P_T) >= 1 - max_{x notin T} t_x.
  VERIFIED: producer 0 failures on 80,000 triples, fires on 5,564 of them
  and on 85.47% of 3,000 random designs; necessary form 0 violations over
  100 triples of 5 boundary systems, worst slack +2.3e-3 (TIGHT -- compare
  the mass form, worst slack +1.3e-1).  LANDED.
  Scalar consequence, verified 0 violations, worst slack +3.6e-2:
      4 t_a t_b t_c [abc]^2  <=  max(t_a,t_b,t_c) * (s_a+s_b+s_c)^2 .

F75B-5.  THE SYMMETRIC CRITERION.
  For the gap N = K_C - 1 of a triple, in campaign currency,
      e1(N) = l_a+l_b+l_c-3 ,  e2(N) = q_ab+q_ac+q_bc ,  e3(N) = tripleGapDet
  and positivity of all three gives strict domination.  So a boundary
  system pays, at EVERY triple,
      l_a+l_b+l_c <= 3   or   q_ab+q_ac+q_bc <= 0   or   tripleGapDet <= 0 ,
  and heaviness collapses the first clause to the all-unit case.  Unlike
  Sylvester's ordered chain this names no atom and no pair.
  VERIFIED against the eigenvalue test: 0 mismatches / 60,000 triples.
  It reproduces the survey's own case split: e2(N) <= 0 forces some pair
  minor in the triple to be <= 0, so a triple is either "contains an
  inadmissible pair" (Question 7.5(b)) or has tripleGapDet <= 0; when all
  fifteen pairs are admissible every triple takes the second branch, which
  is Question 7.5(a).  LANDED.

F75B-6.  A PRODUCER THAT IS SHARP GIVEN (trace, determinant), AND ITS LIMIT.
  If nu_min <= 1 then 4 det K <= nu_min (tr K - nu_min)^2 <= (tr K - 1)^2,
  using that x(tau-x)^2 increases on [0, tau/3] and nu_min <= tr K/3.  So
      tr K >= 3  and  4 [abc]^2 > (l_a+l_b+l_c-1)^2   =>   the triple dominates
  and the condition is SHARP given only the trace and the determinant: for
  any (tau, delta) with 4 delta <= (tau-1)^2 there is a PSD matrix with those
  invariants and nu_min < 1.  MEASURED: 0 failures on 60,000 triples, and it
  fires on some triple of 97.83% of 3,000 random designs.
  *** IT CANNOT BE PUSHED TO 100%. ***  Two independent reasons.
  (a) No producer of strict domination can cover all designs, because
      boundary systems exist (split diamond, split tetrahedron): they have
      NO strictly dominating triple, so every such instrument is silent
      there by construction.  Coverage of designs is the wrong target.
  (b) Against the bracket budget sum_{a<b<c} t_a t_b t_c [abc]^2 = 1, the
      contrapositive gives 1 < sum_C t_a t_b t_c (sum l_C - 1)^2/4, and at
      the uniform point t = 1/6, l = 3 the right side is 320/216 = 1.481.
      No contradiction, and the gap is not small enough to be closed by
      sharpening the trace/determinant step, which is already sharp.
      Closing it needs e2 as well, and with all three invariants the
      criterion IS domination -- the statement itself, not an instrument.
  NOT LANDED: the proof runs through eigenvalues (the ordering nu_min <=
  tr/3 and monotonicity of x(tau-x)^2), which the symmetric-function route
  cannot express -- given (tau, delta) alone, e2 is free.

F75B-7.  NO COUNTEREXAMPLE FOUND, AND WHAT WAS SEARCHED.
  Census: 6 exact boundary systems, 8 inadmissible pairs, ALL collinear,
  max |wedge| over them 3.6e-15.  Consistent with the predecessor's 26.
  Caveat, unchanged and important: every boundary system anyone has built
  HAS a collinear pair, so the census says nothing about primitive ones.
  Directed searches for a violation, all negative:
    - (5,3), minimise max_T lmin subject to q_01 <= -delta and w_01 >= 0.05:
      floors at +5.8e-4 (delta=1e-3) and +5.5e-4 (delta=1e-4) with SIX
      triples active at once -- a Chebyshev point, and the value drifts
      down with optimiser effort (2.4e-4 under least squares), so these
      numbers measure the optimiser, NOT a geometric floor.  Do not quote
      them as a margin.
    - (6,3), maximise the wedge of an inadmissible pair over boundary
      systems, 8 restarts: no feasible point reached.
    - Opening a collinear pair of the split tetrahedron while staying on
      the boundary locus: the optimiser cannot move at all.  Reason found:
      that point has TWELVE simultaneously tight triples (4 tetrahedron
      triples x 2^(number of split atoms they contain)), so it is a highly
      singular point of the locus and a bad start.  Use a generic boundary
      system with a collinear pair instead.
  Generation note: descent on Phi^2 = (max_T lmin)^2 does NOT converge
  (0 systems from many starts).  What works is least squares on the 20
  residuals r_T = max(lmin(S_T - I), 0) with method='trf' -- 'lm' is
  refused, 20 residuals < 24 variables.  Yield about 4 in 129 starts.
