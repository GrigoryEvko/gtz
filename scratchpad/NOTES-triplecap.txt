NOTES-triplecap  --  verdict on the triple cap inequality, and what replaced it
===============================================================================

VERDICT
-------
The conjecture  kappa_ab + kappa_bc + kappa_ca <= 2 pi  is FALSE, and it is false
on REALISABLE data, not only off the constraint set.  It is now refuted in Lean.

Witness (Gtz.lightTrineAtom / Gtz.lightTrineWeight, Gtz/Wave/PlaneWeightFloor.lean):
  three plane atoms of leverage 11/10 at sixty degrees, uniform weight 20/33.
  Gtz.lightTrineFrame            -- Parseval holds exactly
  Gtz.lightTrine_noStrictPair    -- every squared pairing 121/400 beats (l-1)^2 = 1/100
  Gtz.lightTrine_sum_weight      -- total weight 20/11
  Gtz.two_pi_lt_sum_nonStrictCapAngle_lightTrine
      -- the three caps total 6 arccos(1/11) > 2 pi
The unconstrained version fails faster: rho -> pi/2 sends each hypotenuse to pi/2
and the sum to 3 pi/2 > pi.

The equality claim is also false.  Equality  sum kappa = 2 pi  holds on a whole
TWO-parameter family (every three-class rank-two tie), not only at the Mercedes.
The coordinator verified the same family independently, |sum t - 1| = 4.4e-16.

The conjecture is also VACUOUS at the target cell.  Gtz.card_coplanar_le_three_of_
isPrimitiveDesign_of_isTie (Gtz/Wave/TieStratumClassification.lean:310) caps a
coplanar family of a parallel-free (6,3) tie at THREE members, so a four- or
five-member circle stratum does not exist there.  No (6,3) corollary is claimed.

WHAT REPLACED IT -- the plane weight floor, a RANK-TWO law at every size
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
THEOREM (Gtz.one_le_sum_weight_of_planeFrame).  g_a in R^2, t_a > 0 on a finite
support, sum_a t_a g_a g_a^T = I_2 with sigma := sum t_a UNNORMALISED, every
l_a > 1, and no pair with (l_a-1)(l_b-1) > <g_a,g_b>^2.  Then sigma >= 1.
Equivalently Gtz.weighted_mean_leverage_le_two: the weight-average leverage
2/sigma never exceeds two.  The floor is ATTAINED on the two-parameter family, so
no quantitative margin exists and none is claimed.

Three moves, all in Gtz/Wave/PlaneWeightFloor.lean:
 1. Gtz.sum_le_one_of_pairCap -- u_a >= 0 totalling one, A_a <= 1 - u_a,
    A_a A_b <= u_a u_b  ==>  sum A_a <= 1.  At most one index carries A_k > u_k;
    then A_k * sum A <= A_k^2 + u_k(1-u_k) <= A_k by (A_k-u_k)(A_k-(1-u_k)) <= 0.
    Both endpoints of that quadratic are equalities -- that is exactly why the
    floor is attained on a family and not at a point.
 2. Gtz.coShare_planeTriple_aux / Gtz.coShare_mul_planeTriple_aux -- corank one in
    raw plane coordinates.  det(1 - t_x G_x) = det(t_y G_y + t_z G_z) gives
    u_x = t_y t_z J_yz^2, and the saturated cap u_x u_y = t_x t_y b_xy^2 then
    follows from the trace and Lagrange with NO case analysis.
 3. Gtz.exists_planeDependency / Gtz.exists_smaller_planeFrame -- four plane atoms
    are dependent (Sym_2 R^2 is three-dimensional); sliding the weights along the
    dependency keeps Parseval and moves sum_a t_a (l_a - 1) linearly, so the
    support drops without the excess total falling.

THE CORANK-ONE READING, at every rank
-------------------------------------
Gtz.rank_le_two_of_noPlaneStrictPair -- a (k+1,k) design with no pair satisfying
(l_c-1)(l_d-1) > <g_c,g_d>^2 has k <= 2.
Gtz.exists_planeStrictPair_of_three_le_rank -- so EVERY (k+1,k) design with k >= 3
carries a pair that strictly dominates the identity on the plane it spans.  At
k = 2 it does not, and the three-atom rank-two ties are the equality case.

A SECOND, FULLY UNCONDITIONAL LAW -- Gtz/Wave/CoShareBudget.lean
----------------------------------------------------------------
With u_c := 1 - t_c l_c (Gtz.designCoShare), at EVERY weighted design, every rank,
every size and every pair of distinct atoms:
  Gtz.weight_mul_sq_dotProduct_le_coShare_mul :  t_c t_d <g_c,g_d>^2 <= u_c u_d
  Gtz.sum_offDiag_coShareSlack_eq_corank_mul :
      sum_{c != d} (u_c u_d - t_c t_d <g_c,g_d>^2) = (m-k)(m-k-1).
No heaviness, no domination, no tie, no stationarity.  Both are one identity read
twice: Pi := Gtz.coProjection has sum_e Pi_ce^2 = Pi_cc = u_c, so the Cauchy-
Schwarz minor is the pair law, and summing the minors is two trace readings,
(tr Pi)^2 = (m-k)^2 against tr(Pi^T Pi) = tr Pi = m-k.
 * Corank one is the equality case (Gtz.coShareSlack_eq_zero_of_corankOne), a
   second proof of the tree's Gtz.weight_mul_sq_dotProduct_eq_coShare_mul with no
   outer square and no trace-one argument.
 * At (6,3) the budget is SIX over thirty ordered pairs, so some pair of every
   design of the open cell carries slack at least 1/5
   (Gtz.exists_coShareSlack_sixThree).  Hypothesis-free.
 * Gtz.designCoShare_nonneg_ofAnyRank extends the tree's corank-one nonnegativity
   to every rank and size.

WHY IT SURVIVES THE FOUR CAMPAIGN CONSTRAINTS
---------------------------------------------
* Realness: sum_c u_c = m - k is the trace of a REAL projection, and the corank-one
  saturation is a real rank-one minor.  Over C the Veronese space is four-
  dimensional and the triple identity loses the phase.
* No quantitative gap: the floor is attained on a two-parameter family.
* Not spectral: no eigenvalue of sum_c g_c g_c^T appears anywhere.
* Not matroid: the caps read the metric (l_a-1)(l_b-1) <= <g_a,g_b>^2, not a rank
  function.
