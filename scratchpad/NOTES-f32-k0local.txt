F32-6. FIELD SENSITIVITY (the realness question, answered)
-----------------------------------------------------------
THE MIRROR RIGIDITY IS FIELD-BLIND. mirror_reading_smul_eq is a subtraction
of two null conditions and holds verbatim over C with the bilinear form;
the step "(g_p.w) g_p = (g_q.w) g_q and (g_p.w) != 0 => proportional" is
pure linear algebra, valid over any field. So the LOCAL theorem does NOT
need realness, and complextie_c1 does not threaten it.
WHERE REALNESS MUST ENTER: the moment the argument passes from the exact
mirror hypothesis to the QUANTITATIVE one. Over R the wedge is a SUM OF
SQUARES (wedge_nonneg, via crossNormSq), so "wedge <= 0" forces "wedge = 0"
forces parallelism. Over C the bilinear wedge is not a sum of squares, it
vanishes on isotropic atoms with no proportionality, and wedge_nonneg is
FALSE. So the ORDER content of section 5 - and only that - is the real
step. The global law will inherit exactly this: its degeneration factor is
the wedge, and its realness is wedge_nonneg.
