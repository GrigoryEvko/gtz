  Sum_{|T|=r} det(S_T - 1) = Sum_j (-1)^{k+j} C(m-j, r-j) e_j(N),   N = Sum_c g_c g_c^T.
  The tree had this at r = k ONLY (Gtz.sum_det_subsetSum_sub_one_eq).  The shipped proof
  never uses |T| = k; removing that hypothesis is the whole step.  Below the rank the top
  layers vanish because an r-sized Gram block has no j-minor for j > r

  down to r = 2.

      Sum_r z^r Sum_{|T|=r} det(S_T - 1) = (-1)^k (1+z)^{m-k} det(I - z(N - 1)).
  So the whole determinant profile across ALL subset sizes is one degree-m polynomial in
  the k eigenvalues of N - 1.

F-LP-3.  WHY THIS CANNOT CLOSE THE STRATUM, and no successor should try.
  Both the tie bound and the five-set bound carry the SAME coefficient -4 on e_2(N).  The
  e_2 layer is the only place the pair geometry enters (e_2(N) = Sum_{a<b} w_ab by
  Cauchy-Binet), and it CANCELS in the difference of the two hypotheses.  A parallel pair
  is a vanishing 2x2 Gram minor, so the two aggregates see it through one shared
  coefficient and cannot separate it.  Landed as Gtz.e_two_cancels_in_the_window.
  The window is also measurably non-empty: at the split diamond (in the stratum, WITH a
  parallel pair) nu = (4,4,6), triple total -55, five total +224, sigma_1 = 2/3 -- wide
  slack on both.
