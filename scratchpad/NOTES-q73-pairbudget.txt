F75-1.  *** THE PAIR BUDGET -- A NEW EXACT IDENTITY, EVERY SIZE, EVERY PAIR ***
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EVERY weighted design of rank three and EVERY pair a != b:

  sum_{c not in {a,b}} t_c * tripleGapDet(g_a, g_b, g_c)
      = 2*q_ab*(t_a + t_b) - (l_a - 1)*(1 - t_b) - (l_b - 1)*(1 - t_a)

The LEFT side carries the whole design; the RIGHT names only the pair.
DERIVATION: instantiate the landed Gtz.weighted_tripleGapDet_sum_compl at the
subset C = {a,b}^c, so the leftover subset is {a,b} ITSELF.  Its axis moment
evaluates because a pair reads itself at (l_a, p) and (p, l_b):
    pairAxisForm a b (l_a) (p) = -(D*(l_a-1) + 2D + (l_b-1))     [sympy: 0]
    pairAxisForm a b (p) (l_b) = -(D*(l_b-1) + 2D + (l_a-1))     [sympy: 0]
and weightedExcess({a,b}^c) = 2 - t_a(l_a-1) - t_b(l_b-1) by the landed total.
The whole assembly is one ring step [sympy residual 0].
The landed complement identity leaves the axis moment of the COMPLEMENT, which
F68-4 records as the term nobody could bound; taking the subset to be the
pair's own complement leaves the PAIR instead, which evaluates.

F75-12.  *** A REFUTATION I CAUGHT MYSELF: THE REWEIGHTING FAMILY IS UNSOUND ***
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The identity holds at EVERY design, so it is tempting to apply it to the
reweighted atoms h_c = sqrt(t_c/s_c) g_c with weights s.  Those satisfy Parseval
for every positive s summing to one (sum s_c h_c h_c' = sum t_c g_c g_c' = I),
so (h,s) IS a design, and optimising s gives a whole family of certificates.
MEASURED: the family fires at 100.000% on (6,3) where the plain budget fires at
98.667%.
IT IS UNSOUND.  DOMINATION SUMS THE ATOMS UNWEIGHTED -- Dominates D T is
sum_{c in T} g_c g_c' >= I, not sum_{c in T} t_c g_c g_c' >= I -- so the
reweighted design has DIFFERENT dominators and a certificate for (h,s) says
nothing about (g,t).  THE MANDATORY CALIBRATION CAUGHT IT: the (5,3) diamond, a
genuine tie, returns sup over s = +infinity.  Tenth thin-tail event of the
campaign.  RULE RESTATED: run the diamond before believing any 100%.
