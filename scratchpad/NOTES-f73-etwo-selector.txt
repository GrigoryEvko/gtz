CONSEQUENCE FOR THE STATEMENT TO BE PROVED.  Conditions (A) and (B) must be
MERGED into a single existential, and the merged form is exactly what the
contradiction needs:

    (AB)  At a corner there exist an outside pair {d,d'} with q_{dd'} > 0 and an
          inside atom e in C with tripleGapDet(g_d, g_d', g_e) > 0.

Since a triple carrying two or more inside atoms never strictly dominates at a
corner (landed corner_posDef_triple_inter_le_one) and the corner itself does not
dominate strictly, (AB) together with the complement is the whole of

    (E)  a corner design is never a tie
       <=> at a corner, either the complement strictly dominates, or some
           one-inside triple does.

Proving (A) separately and (B) separately is STRICTLY HARDER than proving (AB),
and by the witness above (B) alone is not even true.


F73-1.  THE CORNER-PLUS-PAIR CONFIGURATION IS PARAMETRIZED BY (c, W), AND (B)
        IS NOT A STATEMENT ABOUT IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At a corner the inside Gram is K_C = I + c c' with c in R^3, c_e^2 = l_e - 1,
c_e c_f = <g_e, g_f>, and |c|^2 = lam (the landed axis law, restated).  The three
inside atoms span R^3, so the outside pair is a linear combination of them and
its Gram is FORCED:

    N = Gram(a,b) = W' K_C^{-1} W = W'W - (W'c)(W'c)' / (1 + lam)

with W the 3x2 matrix of readings r_e = (a.g_e, b.g_e).  VERIFIED to 1.332e-14
over 2000 random (c,W); and the repayment matrix diagonal reproduces
tripleGapDet to 1.478e-12 (scratchpad/f73/cw.py).

So the pair {a,b} carries NO data beyond W, and the whole configuration of five
vectors is the pair (c, W) in R^3 x R^{3x2}, free.

*** THEREFORE: over R, on the bare (c,W) family with M = N - I positive
definite, the per-pair statement FAILS at 43.6117% of 166735 admissible
samples, worst scaled margin -9.953581e-01. ***  A counterexample:
    c = [-0.35359882, 1.9195355, -0.41380591]
    W = [[-3.86734746, -1.69452009], [-2.10726177, 3.0877117], [-0.50047654, -1.91874252]]
