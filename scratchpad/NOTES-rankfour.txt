NOTES-rankfour.txt — the rank-four lane (Conjecture 9.2 of the brief: the two remaining
general-rank registry axioms, the window 2k <= m <= k(k+1)/2).

All numbers below are EXACT RATIONALS, computed from the reduced incidence matrix of the
named graph with unit conductances: L = B^T B on rank = V-1 reduced coordinates, projection
P = B L^{-1} B^T (transfer impedance), uniform design weight 1/m. Every harness run was
calibrated first against the landed K4 values and reproduced them exactly.


F-R4-1. CALIBRATION AGAINST THE LANDED K4 CHART. PASSES.
  K4, rank 3, size 6: diagonal 1/2, meeting |P| 1/4, disjoint |P| 0, weight 1/6,
  free excess 1/3, two meeting partners contribute 1/2, deficit 1/6.
  This reproduces Gtz.kFourProfile_excess_eq and Gtz.kFourProfile_deficit_eq exactly.


F-R4-2. THE COMPLETE-GRAPH PROFILE IS EXACT AT EVERY RANK TESTED.
  Gtz.CompleteGraphProfile asks diagonal 2/(rank+1), weight 2/(rank(rank+1)), meeting
  pairing magnitude 1/(rank+1). Measured on K_{rank+1}:

    rank 3  K4  size  6 : diag 1/2   meet 1/4   disjoint 0   weight 1/6   excess 1/3
    rank 4  K5  size 10 : diag 2/5   meet 1/5   disjoint 0   weight 1/10  excess 3/10
    rank 5  K6  size 15 : diag 1/3   meet 1/6   disjoint 0   weight 1/15  excess 4/15

  Every value matches the closed form. So the profile hypothesis of
  Gtz.not_exists_excessDominates_of_edgeLabelling is inhabited at every rank, and the
  rank-four instance Gtz.not_exists_excessDominates_kFive is not vacuous.


F-R4-3. LANDED: CROWDEDNESS AT EVERY RANK (commit 731f0d9,
        Gtz/Wave/MeetingDegreeCrowdedness.lean, 15 theorems, all pins standard).
  A family of two-element edges in which no edge meets two others spans at least 3/2 its
  own size in vertices (Gtz.three_mul_card_le_two_mul_card_biUnion). Induction, not
  decomposition: remove an isolated edge (1 edge, 2 fresh vertices) or a meeting pair
  (2 edges, 3 fresh vertices).
  With rank edges on rank+1 vertices: 3*rank <= 2*(rank+1), so rank <= 2. Hence from rank
  three every selection of rank edges of a graph on rank+1 vertices is crowded
  (Gtz.exists_meets_two_of_three_le_rank).
  THIS CLOSES THE REGISTRY'S NAMED GAP: "the vertex count 3p + 2s <= rank + 1 against
  2p + s = rank is a hypothesis" above rank three. It is now a theorem at every rank, and
  by counting rather than by `decide`. The `decide` over 210 four-subsets of Fin 10 was
  NOT needed.
  Verified by direct enumeration as well: every selection of rank edges is crowded at K4
  (20 selections), K5 (210), K6 (3003), and at every band cell listed in F-R4-4.


F-R4-4. THE REGISTRY'S NAMED SUCCESSOR SHAPE IS REFUTED. THIS IS THE MAIN NEGATIVE
        FINDING OF THE ROUND.
  The registry says: "A weaker profile is necessary, bounded free excess together with a
  meeting-pair floor. Once that profile exists the crowdedness count closes the lane."
  NO SUCH PAIR OF CONSTANTS EXISTS AT ANY BAND CELL. Exact rationals:

    cell                        size  free excess range      meeting |P| range   max disj |P|
    K5 (threshold, rank 4)        10  [3/10, 3/10]           [1/5, 1/5]          0
    K5 - 1 edge      (band)        9  [13/45, 16/45]         [2/15, 4/15]        1/15
    K5 - 2 adjacent  (band)        8  [11/40, 19/40]         [1/10, 2/5]         1/10
    K5 - 2 disjoint  (band)        8  [41/120, 49/120]       [2/15, 4/15]        2/15
    K4 (threshold, rank 3)         6  [1/3, 1/3]             [1/4, 1/4]          0
    K6 (threshold, rank 5)        15  [4/15, 4/15]           [1/6, 1/6]          0
    K6 - 1 edge      (band)       14  [11/42, 17/56]         [1/8, 5/24]         1/24
    K6 - 3 edges     (band)       12  [1/4, 1/2]             [1/12, 5/12]        1/12

  CAP TEST  max excess <= 2 * min meeting level:
    K4  1/3   <= 1/2    TRUE      K5  3/10 <= 2/5   TRUE      K6  4/15 <= 1/3   TRUE
    K5-1  16/45 <= 8/15  FALSE    K5-2adj 19/40 <= 1/5 FALSE  K5-2disj 49/120 <= 8/15 FALSE
    K6-1  17/56 <= 1/4   FALSE    K6-3  1/2 <= 1/6   FALSE
  So the global two-constant profile fails at EVERY band cell tested, at two ranks.

  Note also: at every band cell the DISJOINT pairings are NONZERO (1/15, 1/10, 2/15,
  1/24, 1/12). On the complete graph they vanish. That is the structural reason the
  profile does not survive deletion: the cut space of a proper subgraph couples edges
  that share no vertex.


F-R4-5. EVEN THE PER-SLOT TWO-PARTNER FORM FAILS AT ONE BAND CELL.
  Per selection, ask: is there a crowded slot whose two meeting partners cover its excess?
    landed form  excess <= 2 * min(|P1|,|P2|) :
      TRUE at K4, K5, K6 (the three threshold cells); FALSE at ALL FIVE band cells.
    sum form     excess <= |P1| + |P2|        :
      TRUE at K5-1, K5-2adj, K6-1, K6-3; FALSE at K5 - 2 DISJOINT EDGES (size 8, rank 4).
  So no two-partner criterion of any kind closes the band. The witness cell is K5 less the
  two disjoint edges {3,4} and {1,2}.


F-R4-6. WHAT DOES WORK: THE WHOLE ROW. Landed as Gtz/Wave/RowCoveredRefutation.lean.
  Excess dominance asks EVERY slot's excess to exceed the sum of the absolute pairings in
  its own row. Measured over every selection of every cell above:
    the maximum over slots of (excess - row sum) is NEGATIVE at every selection.
  Worst (i.e. largest, so closest to failing) values of that maximum:
    K4 -1/6    K5 -3/10   K6 -2/5
    K5-1 -8/45   K5-2adj -7/40   K5-2disj -23/120   K6-1 -9/28   K6-3 -19/60
  The K4 figure -1/6 is exactly the landed deficit, which is the calibration.
  So EVERY slot of EVERY selection of EVERY cell tested is row-covered, with margin.
  Landed: Gtz.not_excessDominates_of_row_le (the direct negation, weakest possible
  hypothesis), Gtz.not_excessDominates_of_two_partners_sum (strictly weaker than the
  landed level form, by Gtz.two_partners_sum_of_level),
  Gtz.RowCoveredAtEverySelection and Gtz.not_exists_excessDominates_of_rowCovered.

  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE REGISTRY. The excess-dominance instrument is dead at every rank and
  at every cell of the window, threshold and band alike, and it is now dead by theorem at
  the threshold cells and by exact measurement at the band. A successor must not be a
  two-constant profile; the correct hypothesis is Gtz.RowCoveredAtEverySelection.

  BUT NOTE, AND THIS IS THE LIMIT OF THE WHOLE LANE: none of this proves the axiom.
  ExcessDominatesBlock refutes IsTie outright, so a producer built on it discharges the
  hinge by refuting its own antecedent. Killing the instrument is a graveyard result. The
  axiom still needs a route that assumes a tie and produces a parallel pair.

F-R4-7. THE STRONG TRIANGLE AT (6,3). This is the round's main positive result and it
        is on the MAIN target, not on rank four.

  Landed: Gtz/Wave/StrongTriangleRamsey.lean, 10 theorems, all pins standard.

  THE COMPOSITION. Two things landed the same day:
    (a) Gtz.branchB_exists_strongPair (sibling lane) turns the SIGN of a triple's gap
        determinant into a PAIR inequality: at a branch-B tie every triple has a pair with
        x_i * x_j <= 4 * <g_i,g_j>^2. Call such a pair STRONG. Constant sharp at the
        regular tetrahedron.
    (b) Gtz.exists_monochromaticTriple_of_pairPredicate (landed long ago in
        Gtz/Design/DominationGates.lean) is R(3,3) = 6 in consumable form.
  Together: strongness is a symmetric relation on six atoms, (a) says no triple is
  entirely weak, so the monochromatic triple (b) forces cannot be weak.

  **Gtz.branchB_exists_strong_triangle : a branch-B (6,3) tie carries three atoms that
  are PAIRWISE strong.**

  WHY THIS MATTERS. The campaign's standing verdict is "the graph delivers the family and
  never the sign, because at a tie every live triple has det <= 0 BY HYPOTHESIS". The
  strong-pair theorem is exactly the missing conversion of that hypothesis into a
  pair-local statement, and a pair-local statement is what a graph can count. This is the
  first time the graph programme produces a conclusion rather than a family.

  WHY SIX. R(3,3) = 6 is sharp: at five atoms the pentagon splits the ten pairs into two
  triangle-free halves (Gtz.exists_cliqueFree_three_and_compl_cliqueFree_three_five, in
  the tree), so no monochromatic triangle is forced and the argument fails at exactly the
  size where the hinge itself is FALSE (Gtz.not_hingeHoldsAtSize_five_three, the diamond).
  This is a second, combinatorial, reason for the five-six gap, independent of the conic
  count of Gtz.sizeGap_at_rank_three. Worth putting in the survey.

  THE PRICE IN WEIGHTS. Strongness is about directions and Parseval is about weights; the
  landed pairing cap Gtz.pairing_cap joins them. With a_c = t_c * (l_c - 1) the weighted
  excess and s_c = t_c * l_c the share:
      Gtz.strongPair_weight_cap        a_i * a_j <= 4 * (1 - s_i) * (1 - s_j)
      Gtz.strongTriangle_weight_product_cap   (a_i a_j a_k)^2 <= (8 (1-s_i)(1-s_j)(1-s_k))^2
  Both right sides are positive because Gtz.share_lt_one_of_isTie gives s_c < 1 at a tie.
  These are the first inequalities tying the strong-pair geometry to the budgets
  Sum a = 2, Sum s = 3, Sum t = 1.

  HONEST LIMIT, CHECKED BY HAND: these caps do NOT close branch B. At the uniform point
  t = 1/6, a = 1/3, s = 1/2 the cap reads 1/9 <= 1 and the admissible-row leverage floor
  Gtz.leverageExcess_floor_of_admissibleRow reads 1/3 > 5/48 -- both satisfied with a wide
  margin. The strong triangle is a structural restriction, not a contradiction. What is
  missing is a LOWER bound on the triangle's excesses strong enough to meet the cap.

  A FREE BY-PRODUCT, no branch hypothesis:
      Gtz.exists_admissible_or_inadmissible_triangle -- every six-atom rank-three design
      has an all-admissible or an all-inadmissible triangle (pure Ramsey on admissibility).
      Gtz.isTie_allHeavy_inadmissible_triangle_or_strongPair -- at an all-heavy (6,3) tie,
      either some triple is entirely inadmissible, or some triple is entirely admissible
      and carries a strong pair.
  The second is a case split for the WHOLE all-heavy branch, not just branch B, and the
  inadmissible-triangle alternative hands Question 7.5(b) three inadmissible pairs at once
  rather than one.
