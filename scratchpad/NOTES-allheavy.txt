# The all-heavy residual at (6,3): the circuit laws of the dependency criterion

Lane: JOB A (dependency criterion on the residual), with readings for JOB B and JOB D.
Landed module: Gtz/Wave/CircuitSwapLaw.lean, 1032 lines, 31 pinned theorems, all
[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound].

## 1. What is new

The landed criterion Gtz.dominates_iff_dependencyBound_compl reads domination on the
dependency space. At (6,3) that space has dimension three, and the twenty triples give
twenty comparisons of quadratic forms on it. This lane writes those comparisons down at
the dependencies that Cramer supplies for free, the CIRCUITS of the four-subsets.

A circuit of a four-set S is supported on four labels. Two cases occur.

  * S contains the triple C. Then exactly one label of the support lies outside C, so
    the right side of the criterion is a SINGLE term. This gives the ONE-ATOM SWAP LAW.
  * S meets C in a pair. Then two labels lie on each side. This gives the PAIR SWAP LAW.

In the volume currency of Gtz/Wave/TwentyVolumeMarginals.lean, with th_x = t_x/(1 - t_x):

  ONE-ATOM SWAP LAW   (Gtz.swapLaw_volume_of_dominates)
      Dominates {a,b,c}  =>   th_a nu_bcd + th_b nu_acd + th_c nu_abd  <=  nu_abc .

  PAIR SWAP LAW       (Gtz.pairSwapLaw_volume_of_dominates)
      Dominates {a,b,c}  =>   th_a nu_bde + th_b nu_ade  <=  nu_abd + nu_abe .

The first says a dominating triple's own mass dominates the three masses got by swapping
one member out for d, each discounted by the odds of the member that leaves. The second
carries NO weight on the right, and the third member c enters only through the
hypothesis. Both are new.

The module also lands the STRICT twin of the criterion, which the campaign did not have
(Gtz.posDef_gap_iff_dependencyBound_compl), and its contrapositive
Gtz.exists_dependency_reversal_of_isTie: at a tie EVERY k-subset admits a nonzero
dependency at which the criterion reverses.

The aggregate over the three outside labels of a (6,3) design collapses the nine swapped
masses onto the three pair masses inside the dominator
(Gtz.aggregate_swapLaw_of_dominates_sixThree):

      th_a delta_bc + th_b delta_ac + th_c delta_ab  <=  (3 + th_a + th_b + th_c) nu_abc .

This is the TRACE reading of the criterion at the dominator. It couples the pair layer of
the determinantal measure to the triple layer through DOMINATION, and not through
Hadamard.

## 2. Measurements, in double precision, scratchpad/circuit

Criterion against direct domination: 200000 random (6,3) designs, all 4000000 triples,
ZERO mismatches, weakly and strictly. This reproduces the predecessor's 0 of 10800 and
extends it, and it validates the strict form before it was proved.

One-atom swap law: 829894 dominating triples times three outside labels, ZERO violations.
Sharp: over a second run of 1663068 dominating triples the largest ratio of the two sides
is 0.999088.

Pair swap law: 3739563 instances, ZERO violations, largest ratio 0.998715.

The law is strictly WEAKER than domination. 267296 of 1097190 triples that pass all three
one-atom swap conditions do not dominate. This is expected: the swap law is the DIAGONAL
of the matrix criterion K^T diag(1/(1-t_C)) K <= diag(1/t_{C'}), where K holds the
coordinates of the outside atoms in the basis C, and a matrix inequality is not implied by
its diagonal.

Determinant reading, MEASURED and NOT proved: nu_{C'} th_a th_b th_c <= nu_C at every
dominating triple, largest ratio 0.441, zero violations in 1663068 instances. It is the
determinant of the same matrix inequality. A proof needs 0 <= A <= B => det A <= det B,
which the tree does not carry.

## 4. JOB B: the four pairs of 2.2 and 2.3 do NOT collide

Measured over 400000 random (6,3) designs, of which 142136 (35.5 percent) are all-heavy.

  max over designs of min_pair delta      = 0.146803   against the sharp ceiling 1/5
  max over designs of min_pair delta'     = 0.153813   against the sharp ceiling 1/5
  max over designs of min_pair (s_p+s_q)  = 0.978309   against the ceiling 1
  min over designs of max_pair (s_p+s_q)  = 1.028844   against the floor   1

The pair of least mass and the pair of least cap slack are the SAME pair in only 7746 of
400000 designs, that is 1.9 percent. The share sum at the least-mass pair ranges over
[0.0048, 1.0820] and exceeds one in 4084 designs, so that pair can be light or heavy.

Conclusion. The four distinguished pairs are four DIFFERENT pairs and nothing forces them
together. The 1/5 ceilings are attained only at the icosahedron chart, which random
sampling does not reach, so the pigeonhole has slack of about 25 percent at a typical
design. This branch is a NEGATIVE and no successor should spend a cycle on it.

## 5. Why the aggregates cannot close the stratum

Every aggregate of the swap law carries a factor that DEGRADES with the weight floor. The
one-atom law read at a corner, where all three inside minors vanish and the inside Gram is
an orthonormal basis stretched by sqrt(1+lam) along the axis, gives

      (l_b+l_c-1)/(1-t_a) + (l_a+l_c-1)/(1-t_b) + (l_a+l_b-1)/(1-t_c)
        <=  (3 + th_a + th_b + th_c)(1 + lam) .

With the corner trace law sum_C (l - 1) = lam the left side is at least 3 + 2 lam and the
right side is at least 3 + 3 lam, so the relation is VACUOUS at every corner. This agrees
with the standing warning that no certificate with an absolute constant can exist, and it
says the same for this instrument.

The negative-association readings are equally weak. From the aggregate and the landed
nu_C <= delta_{pq} s_r one gets only s_a >= t_a/(3 - 2 t_a), which is weaker than
all-heaviness.

## 6. What is left, and where the instrument still has room

The swap law is the DIAGONAL of the criterion at a dominating triple. Two readings of the
same matrix inequality are NOT landed and are the obvious next step.

  * The DETERMINANT reading nu_{C'} th_a th_b th_c <= nu_C, measured at 0.441 with zero
    violations. It couples a dominating triple to its COMPLEMENT, so it speaks to both
    halves of the hinge dictionary at once. It needs the monotonicity of the determinant
    on the positive semidefinite order, which is not in the tree.
  * The OFF-DIAGONAL readings, which come from dependencies that are combinations of two
    circuits. A combination that kills one coefficient of the dominator gives a two
    against two comparison in bracket vocabulary. These are the entries the diagonal
    misses, and the measurement that 24 percent of swap-law-passing triples do not
    dominate is exactly the size of that gap.

Complexification. The criterion, the Cramer law and both swap laws survive passage to C
with Hermitian squares in place of squares. So this instrument alone CANNOT prove the
hinge, by the standing rule. It is a domination instrument, not a hinge instrument, and it
should be spent on emptying strata rather than on producing a parallel pair.
