F49-9.  WHY A DIRECT POSITIVSTELLENSATZ ATTACK IS OUT OF RANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------
Refuting {corner, full Parseval, all nine R <= 0} means eliminating over
6 atoms x 3 coords + 6 weights = 24 parameters, less 3 for rotation, with the
6 Parseval equations: ~15 effective dimensions at degree ~6.  The K2 chart
capstone succeeded at 5 variables, degree 6, on a POLYTOPE.  This is far outside
that range.  Any tractable attack must first cut the problem down -- e.g. by a
partial selection rule that leaves a small residual cell -- not certify the
disjunction whole.
