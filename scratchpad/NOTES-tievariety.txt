THE TIE VARIETY IN THE DUAL (w) CHART
=====================================
Lane: exact classification of the tie stratum.  All numbers below are MEASURED
in double precision unless the line says PROVED.

1. THE CHART, AND THE CRITERION IT GIVES (PROVED)
-------------------------------------------------
V is the m x k matrix with rows sqrt(t_c) g_c^T, so Parseval is V^T V = I_k.
P := V V^T is a rank-k projection, Q := I - P has rank m-k.  Write
    s_c := 1 - t_c,   Q = U U^T with U^T U = I_{m-k},   w_c := u_c / sqrt(s_c).
Then  sum_c s_c w_c w_c^T = I_{m-k}  and  sum_c s_c = m - 1.

  THE CRITERION.  For every k-subset C,
      C dominates        <=>  sum_{c in C} w_c w_c^T  <=  I_{m-k}
      C dominates strictly <=> sum_{c in C} w_c w_c^T  <   I_{m-k}.

  PROOF.  The landed Gtz.dominates_iff_posSemidef_projectionBlock_finset gives
  C dominates <=> P[C,C] - diag(t)_C >= 0.  Now
  P[C,C] - diag(t)_C = diag(s)_C - U_C U_C^T, and for A > 0,
  A - B B^T >= 0 <=> B^T A^{-1} B <= I.  Here B^T A^{-1} B = sum_{c in C} w_c w_c^T.
  Weight positivity gives s_c > 0 (t_c < 1 needs m >= 2).

  EQUIVALENT FORM.  With N := sum_c t_c w_c w_c^T (> 0 always),
      C dominates <=> sum_{c NOT in C} w_c w_c^T >= N.
  At corank one this is exactly the landed Gtz.dominates_erase_of_coMean_le:
  |w_c|^2 is the landed coRatio R_c and tr N is the landed coMean Sigma.

  MEASURED: 0 mismatches out of 60000 subsets over (5,3),(6,3),(7,3),(8,4),(6,4).

  DUALITY.  ghat_c := N^{-1/2} w_c gives a weighted design at (m, m-k) with the
  SAME weights, and C dominates in D <=> C^c dominates in ghat, weak and strict.
  So IsTie transports.  This is the sharp involution, with an explicit formula.

  A PARALLEL PAIR, READ IN THE CHART.  D has g_p parallel to g_q  <=>  the m-2
  vectors {w_c : c not in {p,q}} have rank <= m-k-1.  At (5,3): the complementary
  TRIPLE of w's is pairwise parallel.  At (6,3): the complementary FOUR-set of
  w's is coplanar.  This matches the landed pairCapSlack dictionary.

2. THE (4,3) FIXTURE REPRODUCES THE LANDED CORANK-ONE LAW
---------------------------------------------------------
1573 ties of 2000 restarts.  dim of the design variety is k(m-k) + m - 1 = 6.
Tie locus: codim 3, DIM 3 = the weight simplex.  All four triples tight,
|w_c|^2 = 1/3 at every atom, parallel-free, weights strongly non-uniform.
Leverage law k t_c l_c - (k-1) - t_c = 0 to 1e-10.  This is
Gtz.isTie_iff_leverage_law, recovered.

  FIVE-LINE PROOF OF IT IN THE CHART.  At corank one w_c is a scalar, a_c := w_c^2.
  No strict dominator gives a_p <= sum_d t_d a_d for every p; a weak dominator
  gives a_{p0} >= sum_d t_d a_d.  A t-weighted mean with all t_d > 0 that is
  bounded below by every term forces all a_d equal; sum_c s_c a_c = 1 pins
  a_c = 1/k, i.e. k t_c l_c = (k-1) + t_c.

3. THE (5,3) TIE VARIETY  [MEASURED, 3746 ties of 6000 restarts]
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Design variety dim 10.  EVERY tie found has codim 6, so

    DIM OF THE (5,3) TIE LOCUS = 4 = dim of the open weight simplex.

Exactly TWO combinatorial types, by the partition of the five labels into the
parallel classes of the w vectors -- always exactly THREE classes:

  type (1,2,2): 8 of 10 triples dominate, all exactly tight   [the DIAMOND]
  type (1,1,3): 7 of 10 triples dominate, all exactly tight

  A triple dominates <=> its complementary PAIR is not inside one class, and
  then it is exactly tight.  The three-class structure is forced by the landed
  Gtz.rankTwoFourDirectionHinge_holds carried across the duality of section 1.

  THE LAW.  Let x_c be the leverage of the sharp dual atom and T_j the total
  weight of class J_j.  Then, to 6.4e-11 and 3.9e-12 over 1858 ties,
      x_c is CONSTANT on each class, and   x_c = (1 + T_j) / (2 T_j)  for c in J_j,
  i.e. the merged (3,2) design obeys the corank-one law 2 T L = 1 + T.
  The weights are FREE: the map (tie locus) -> (weight simplex) has full rank 4,
  and the observed weights cover (0.006, 0.85) in every coordinate.

  SO THE DIAMOND IS NOT ISOLATED.  It is one point of a 4-parameter family, the
  type-(1,2,2) component, and its weights can be moved anywhere in the simplex.
  Up to relabelling there are exactly two components; with labels, 25 = S(5,3).

4. THE (6,3) TIE VARIETY  [MEASURED, 4920 ties of 12000 restarts]
------------------------------------------------------------------
Design variety dim 14.  EVERY tie found has codim 9, so

    DIM OF THE (6,3) TIE LOCUS = 5 = dim of the open weight simplex,

and EVERY ONE OF THE 4920 TIES CARRIES A PARALLEL PAIR (the four complementary
w vectors are coplanar to between 3.7e-17 and 1.4e-14).  Four combinatorial
types by the w-parallel-class partition, all of dimension 5:
  (1,1,1,1,2) with 13 tight triples ; (1,1,1,1,2) with 12 ; (1,1,2,2) with 12 ;
  (1,1,1,3) with 10.

  THE PARALLEL PAIR IS A DUPLICATE.  Over 12000 further ties, at three seeds and with
  weight floors 0 and 0.03, the worst max cos^2 between two atoms is 1.00000000000000 and
  the length ratio |g_p|/|g_q| of that pair is 1.0000000000 at min, median and max.  So the
  parallel pair is not merely parallel: THE TWO ATOMS ARE EQUAL, which is exactly
  Gtz.duplicateSplitDesign, whose tie transfer Gtz.isTie_duplicateSplitDesign_iff is an iff
  already landed.  Merging that pair returned an exact (5,3) tie in 400 of 400 tests, worst
  |value - 1| = 8.8e-16.

  WHY 5 IS THE NUMBER THE HINGE PREDICTS.  A (6,3) tie with a parallel pair
  merges to a (5,3) tie (landed).  The (5,3) tie locus has dim 4 and its weights
  are free; a split re-divides one weight, adding exactly ONE parameter.  So the
  split locus has dim 4 + 1 = 5.  The measured (6,3) tie locus has dim 5 and is
  covered by parallel pairs.  Dimensions AGREE, which is the outcome the hinge
  predicts and the outcome a counterexample would have to violate.


5. WHY THE SPLIT LOCUS IS FIVE-DIMENSIONAL AND NOT SIX
-------------------------------------------------------
A parallel split of one atom of a (5,3) tie has TWO free parameters: the weight share and
the length ratio r (the constraints are tau_p + tau_q = t_c and tau_p |g_p|^2 + tau_q
|g_q|^2 = t_c |g_c|^2).  Scanning that whole plane at the diamond, the split is an exact
tie ONLY at r = +-1, at every share, and the value rises smoothly away from it:

    tau/t1 = 0.3 :  r = +-1.00 -> value 3.2e-16 ;  +-1.25 -> 0.227 ;  +-1.50 -> 0.391
                    +-0.75 -> 0.108 ;  +-0.50 -> 0.199 ;  +-0.25 -> 0.260

18 ties out of a 72-point (tau, r) grid, and every one of them at r = +-1.  So the ratio is
PINNED and only the share survives: the split locus is 4 + 1 = 5.  This is the same fact as
section 4's measurement that the parallel pair of a (6,3) tie always has length ratio one.

6. THE CONTRAST BETWEEN THE TWO CELLS, IN ONE LINE
---------------------------------------------------
Same code, same objective, same chart, same weight floor:

    (5,3):  935 ties, 531 of them PARALLEL-FREE (worst max cos^2 = 0.2667)
    (6,3): 12000+ ties, ZERO parallel-free (worst max cos^2 = 1.00000000000000)

The (5,3) hinge is false and the search sees that immediately.  At (6,3) the same search
never once leaves the parallel locus.

7. THE ADVERSARIAL SEARCH FOR A PARALLEL-FREE (6,3) TIE
--------------------------------------------------------
Residual = the ten (twenty) tie defects TOGETHER with a barrier max(cos^2_pq - (1-eps), 0),
cos^2_pq = P_pq^2/(P_pp P_qq), under a hard weight floor 0.02, 2500 restarts at each of
eps = 0.30, 0.10, 0.03, 0.01.  Best total loss 2.2e-11 at every eps, and at the optimum:

  * the BARRIER IS INACTIVE (max cos^2 reaches only 0.458 of the 0.70 to 0.99 allowed),
  * TEN triples have f_C > 0, max f_C = 2.7e-6, so the point is NOT a tie but a design with
    ten strictly dominating triples of tiny margin,
  * four of the six weights sit exactly ON the floor.

So the infimum is approached by degenerating the weights, not by finding a parallel-free
tie, and the barrier never binds.  Without the floor the same search runs the weights to
1e-6 and reports 6e-13 -- that is the failure mode the brief warns about, and the floor is
what exposes it.

8. WHAT IS PROVED, WHAT IS COMPUTED
------------------------------------
PROVED (in Lean, Gtz/Wave/DependencyDominationCriterion.lean, standard axioms):
  the block-versus-kernel criterion at a symmetric idempotent, and its design corollaries
  Gtz.dominates_iff_kernelBound, Gtz.dominates_iff_dependencyBound and
  Gtz.dominates_iff_dependencyBound_compl.
PROVED on paper, not yet in Lean: the w-chart criterion of section 1 and its corank-one
  specialisation (section 2), and the sharp duality ghat = N^{-1/2} w.
COMPUTED, in double precision, NOT proved: everything in sections 3 to 7 -- the (5,3)
  classification, the dimensions 4 and 5, the class law x_c = (1+T_j)/(2T_j), and the
  statement that every (6,3) tie carries a parallel pair.  A search that finds no
  counterexample is not a proof that none exists, and a component of the (6,3) tie variety
  of dimension 4 or less would be invisible to random restarts.
