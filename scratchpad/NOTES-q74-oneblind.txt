Question 7.4 of the brief: the rank-two case of a (6,3) boundary system with
EXACTLY ONE blind member.  Everything below is reproducible from the normal
form in section 1; the scripts themselves are gitignored, so the numbers are
transcribed verbatim.

CALIBRATION (run first, per the standing rule).  The (5,3) diamond reproduces
exactly: Parseval residual 6.66e-16, leverages (2, 3.25, 3.25, 3.25, 3.25),
distinct pair minors {0.75, 2.0, 4.5}, 8 of 10 dominating triples.


================================================================================
1. THE NORMAL FORM.  The case is SOLVED, not merely constrained.
================================================================================

Setup: C = {a,b,c} dominates, rank(S_C - I) = 2, unit kernel w, and
<g_a,w> = 0 while be := <g_b,w> and ga := <g_c,w> are nonzero.

Choose coordinates with w = e_3 and rotate the plane so that the shared plane
direction is e_1.  Then the whole case is

    g_a = (a1, a2, 0)
    g_b = (ga*n, 0, be)
    g_c = (-be*n, 0, ga)        with be^2 + ga^2 = 1.

FOUR parameters (a1, a2, n, theta) for the inside triple, and every condition of
the case holds IDENTICALLY -- none is imposed:

    S_C w = w                     max deviation 2.22e-16 over 4000 draws
    <g_a,w> = 0                   exactly 0
    pairGapMinor(g_b,g_c) = 0     max deviation 5.33e-15
    S_C = diag(M M^T, 1),  M = [[a1, n],[a2, 0]]      max deviation 7.11e-15
    e2 = (a2^2 - 1)(n^2 - 1) - a1^2                   max deviation 4.80e-14

The last line is worth stating twice: the second invariant of the triple is a
SINGLE PAIR MINOR of the two plane vectors g_a and n.  That is the content
landed as Gtz.blind_pairMinorTotal_eq_pairGapMinor (commit fef482a), together
with the antiparallel law and the two decompositions

    g_b = be*w + ga*n ,   g_c = ga*w - be*n .

Domination is equivalent to M M^T > I_2 in the plane, i.e. BOTH singular values
of M exceed one; equivalently a2^2 > 1, n^2 > 1, (a2^2-1)(n^2-1) > a1^2.  So
leverageOf g_a > 1 and |n|^2 > 1 are automatic, never hypotheses.

PARAMETER COUNT, confirmed by construction, not by a Jacobian estimate:
  4  inside triple (a1, a2, n, theta)
  3  weights t_a, t_b, t_c
  3  the orthogonal matrix Q completing the outside atoms
  2  the two free outside weight ratios
 -1  the residual rotation gauge of the plane, already fixed by n = (n,0)
 == 11 + 1 (the total-weight normalisation is already spent) = 12.
This is the twelve the brief reports.  Outside atoms are recovered from
R = I - sum_inside t g g^T by g_x = R^{1/2} q_x / sqrt(t_x), which is exact:
Parseval residual 1e-15 on every completed design.

FOUR OF THE TWENTY TRIPLES ARE REFUSED FOR FREE, with no computation: pairGapMinor(g_b,g_c) = 0,
so by the Sylvester chain no triple containing both b and c is positive definite.
Those are {a,b,c} and {b,c,x} for the three outside x.  SIXTEEN triples remain,
matching the brief's "five of sixteen refusals".


================================================================================
2. THE SHARP CONSTANT DOES NOT EXIST.  Every reported value was the region's
   diameter, not a property of the geometry.
================================================================================

Objective: Psi := max over the 16 live triples of lambda_min(S_T - I).  A tie of
this case needs Psi <= 0; the case is empty iff min Psi > 0.

(a) Nelder-Mead directly on Psi, 60 starts, floor tau = 0.05:  min Psi = 0.0501.
(b) The same across floors (26 starts each) -- note the ratio wanders, which is
    itself the signature of under-convergence:

      tau     min Psi     min Psi / tau
      0.12    0.10024     0.835
      0.10    0.08937     0.894
      0.05    0.05704     1.141
      0.02    0.02439     1.220
      0.01    0.02124     2.124        <- visibly unconverged (margins not tied)

(c) Reformulated as the ZERO SET of sum over live triples of max(lambda_min,0)^2
    -- the recommended technique -- the same floors give margins
    0.00191 (tau=0.05), 0.00204 (tau=0.01), 0.00111 (tau=0.001):
    already 25x below (b).

(d) Least squares on the hinge residual vector, tau = 0.001, 48 s:
      cost 1.324e-09, max live margin +0.0000361984.

So the margin fell 0.42 -> ... -> 0.0281 (three earlier lanes) -> 0.0501 -> 0.0011
-> 0.000036 as the method improved, with no sign of a floor.  CONCLUSION: the
sequence of "sharp constants" in the record measures optimizer effort, not the
problem.  There is no constant to report and none should be quoted again.

THE MINIMISING SEQUENCE RUNS TO THE BOUNDARY.  At the best point of (d):
  weights   [0.08034943, 0.001, 0.001, 0.91355572, 0.00309485, 0.001]
  leverages [8.58924, 1.29214, 2.67703, 1.06203, 215.80064, 667.79952]
Three weights sit exactly ON the floor and two leverages have blown up, with the
products t*l bounded (0.667, 0.668, 0.970, 0.690) as the weight cap requires.
An earlier deep polish at the same floor gave the same shape:
  weights   [0.001, 0.1410228, 0.001, 0.001, 0.001, 0.8549772]
  leverages [1.14533, 4.12081, 2.84102, 519.49607, 966.17875, 1.08682]
This is the dust ray: weights to zero, leverages to infinity, t*l finite.


================================================================================
3. THE ESCAPE IS THE JOINT DEGENERATION, NOT EITHER HALF ALONE.
================================================================================

Capping the leverages alone does NOT restore a margin (tau = 0.001, 11 s each):

      leverage cap    best max live margin    max leverage   min weight
        8              0.000369                8.0            at floor
       20              0.000168               20.0            at floor
       60              0.000942               56.4            at floor
       none            0.000385              678.9            at floor

In every row the minimiser pins weights at the floor.  But capping BOTH -- a
compact region -- gives a clearly positive margin that tracks the region:

      tau     leverage cap    best max live margin
      0.02        6            +0.02437992
      0.01       10            +0.00205957
      0.002      40            +0.00028239

CONCLUSION.  On every compact sub-region the case is numerically empty with a
strictly positive margin, and the margin goes to zero only as the region opens
up in BOTH directions at once -- weights to zero and leverages to infinity
together.  Consequences for anyone who works this case next:

  * The case is empty, but the infimum of the margin over the open region is 0.
    A proof therefore CANNOT be a strict inequality with a positive constant,
    and must consume positivity of the weights in an essential way.  This is the
    same shape the cells lane and the E1 lane reached independently.
  * Do not run another descent to "improve the constant".  There is no constant.
  * The obstruction to feasibility is NOT the two closed conditions of the case
    (they are solved exactly by the normal form of section 1); it is the joint
    refusal of the sixteen live triples.

NOT ESTABLISHED, and stated as such: I did not prove emptiness.  The evidence is
numerical and the minimising sequence leaves every compact set, which is exactly
the situation in which numerics cannot decide.  A proof needs the weight cap
t*l <= 1 fed against the sixteen refusals in the normal-form coordinates.
