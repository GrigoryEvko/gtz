F68-1.  *** THE REPAYMENT MATRIX -- THE THREE DETERMINANTS ARE A DIAGONAL ***
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
F49-13 proved the corner and its pair determine e1 of the three one-inside gap
determinants and NOTHING else, and concluded that only the sum law is available.
That conclusion was about the DIAGONAL.  The right object is the MATRIX.

For a corner C and an outside pair (a,b) put   X := D*(K_C - I) - W adj(M) W'
with D = pairGapMinor(a,b), M the pair gap block, W the 3x2 matrix of readings
r_e = (a.g_e, b.g_e).  Then diag(X)_e = tripleGapDet(a,b,g_e).  VERIFIED to
5.710e-13 over 30000 corners.

ITS THREE INVARIANTS ARE ALL CORNER-PAIR DATA (verified, 30000 corners):
   tr X   = (lam-2)D - T - lam*Phi_M(gam)                      3.037e-12
   e2(X)  = D*( |al x be|^2 - 2 lam D - lam T + lam Phi_M(gam) ) 8.715e-09
   det X  = lam * D^2 * [u,a,b]^2                              7.231e-09
   number of POSITIVE eigenvalues of X                         <= 1, always
The last two are the new content.  det X is a PERFECT SQUARE, and by
Cauchy-Binet it factors into the AMBIENT brackets: the axis against the outside
pair, times the dominator's own bracket.  So e2 and e3 DO exist as corner-pair
invariants -- they belong to the matrix, not to its diagonal, which is exactly
why F49-13's two corners share e1 and differ in the diagonal's e2 and e3.

WHY AT MOST ONE POSITIVE EIGENVALUE.  A corner's inside gap K_C - I is RANK ONE
(the landed axis law), and adj(M) is positive semidefinite on an admissible
pair with BOTH atoms heavy, so X <= D*(K_C - I), a rank-one cap.  This is the
corner's own fingerprint: at a general inside triple the cap has rank three and
the statement is empty.

CONSEQUENCE -- THE GAUGE IS SCHUR-HORN.  The inside-frame gauge Vc -> Vc*Q acts
on X by conjugation, so the SPECTRUM is fixed and only the DIAGONAL moves.  E2*
on a pair asks whether the design's own gauge puts a positive entry on the
diagonal of a matrix with signature (1,2).  That is F60-2/F60-4's measured
46.9% flip, explained structurally.

A CORRECTION MADE WHILE PROVING IT: pairAxisPolar_neg_nonneg needs BOTH atoms

F68-5.  A FOURTH CONJUGATION INCIDENT, CAUGHT BY THE DOMINATION CONTROL
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
My first complex evaluation of X used  W*adjM*W'  (which is r_e^T adjM conj(r_e))
instead of  conj(W)*adjM*transpose(W)  (which is r_e^* adjM r_e).  It reported
POSITIVE diagonal entries at the complex tie, i.e. a spurious E2*.  The
domination control -- comparing against det(K_T - I) computed independently --
caught it: the true diagonals are all negative.  RULE, restated: never evaluate
a Hermitian closed form without the control in the same script.  accept.jl had
the control and was right; scan.jl did not and was wrong.

F68-7.  *** ITEM 2 ANSWERED: THE REAL-ONLY INGREDIENT IS BARGMANN EXTREMALITY ***
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F49-18 killed the SIGN form of the 3-cycle.  The MAGNITUDE form is alive, and it
separates decisively at the rebuilt witness.  For a triple write
    tau := rho_ij rho_jk rho_ki ,   deficit := 1 - (Re tau)^2 / (|rho_ij|^2 |rho_jk|^2 |rho_ki|^2)
Over R the pairings are real, tau is real, and the deficit is IDENTICALLY ZERO --
it is an equation, not an inequality.  Over C only Re(tau) enters det(K_T - I)
and the deficit is strictly positive unless the phase happens to align.

HOW IT MUST COMPOSE WITH F68-1, which is the part that is new.  The closing
instrument is a selector for the single positive eigendirection of the repayment
matrix X.  Extremality is an EQUATION on each triple's cross term, so it pins

an interval.  A successor should ask whether the nine one-inside diagonal entries

eigendirection in each.  That is a finite sign system over the chirotope, not a
search, and it is the first formulation of E2* in which realness enters as an
equation rather than as an inequality to be estimated.
