F74-2.  THE FIRST SYLVESTER SIGN IS A COUNT.                  LANDED 377bd15
  At most k labels have rho_c > 1.  Each label spends (1-t_c) rho_c + t_c >= 0,
  the total is k+1 (descent identity + weight sum), and rho_c > 1 is exactly
  "spends more than one".  Hence >= m-k labels clear sign 1, and a selection of
  k of them exists exactly when m >= 2k -- THE CAMPAIGN'S WINDOW FLOOR,
  recovered from the first Sylvester sign.
  SHARP: minimum observed #{rho <= 1} equals m-k exactly at
     (6,3) 3,  (5,3) 2,  (7,3) 4,  (6,4) 2,  (5,2) 3.
  Two new budgets in the same module:
     sum_{a,b} (1-t_a)(1-t_b) Pi_ab^2 = k          (projection law, contracted)
     sum_{a,b} (1-t_a)(1-t_b) sigma_ab = (m-1-k)^2 - k    ; = 1 at (6,3)
  where sigma_ab = (1-rho_a)(1-rho_b) - Pi_ab^2 is the resolvent pair minor.

F74-8.  PRIOR ART: ALREADY ON RECORD, AND THE MECHANISM CONFIRMED
  Skeleton/Obligations.lean already records: "restricted invertibility
  (Bourgain-Tzafriri, Vershynin, BSS, MSS) is VACUOUS at k = d, which is exactly
  this cell; nothing published discharges it."  I did not redo the search.  The
  precise mechanism: in the Spielman-Srivastava normalisation with
  sum_i v_i v_i^T = I_k one has ||V||_F^2 = k and ||V||^2 = 1, so the theorem
  needs l < k and its factor (1 - sqrt(l/k))^2 is exactly 0 at l = k.
  TWO FURTHER DISTINCTIONS worth recording:
   (a) restricted invertibility wants a UNIFORM lower bound on lambda_min;
       our target is a WEIGHT-MATCHED Loewner bound P[T,T] >= diag(t_T), which
       is a different question even at l < k;
   (b) at l = k the quantity with a budget is the DETERMINANT, not lambda_min:
       Cauchy-Binet gives sum_T det(V_T)^2 = 1 (= the landed bracket budget
       sum t_a t_b t_c [abc]^2 = 1), and lambda_min(P[T,T]) >= det(P[T,T])
       because the other two eigenvalues of a projection block are <= 1.
       Pigeonhole then gives some triple with lambda_min >= 1/20.  That is a
       genuine unconditional producer but it is STRICTLY WEAKER than the landed
       scalar floor (96.45%), so I did not land it.
