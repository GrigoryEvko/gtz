after choosing the plane basis, EXACTLY

    g_x = (1, 0, 0)
    g_y = (0,  c, mu*sn)
    g_z = (0,-sn, mu*c )        c^2 + sn^2 = 1,  mu > 1,  lam = mu^2 - 1

and then S_C = diag(1,1,mu^2) IDENTICALLY -- corner residual 0, not 1e-13.
Derivation: h_x = 0 and sum_C h_e p_e = 0 make p_y, p_z ANTI-PARALLEL, so
p_x p_x^T + (1+r^2) p_y p_y^T = I_2 forces p_x unit and p_y perpendicular to it.
Z1 is sn != 0 (g_y reads the axis) and c != 0 (g_z reads it).

SAMPLER, 100 percent acceptance, Parseval exact by construction:
  pick mu, theta, and the six weights;

    S_{{e} u K\d} - 1  =  F_d  -  sum_{c in C, c != e} g_c g_c^T .

H-7.  WHAT IS OPEN, AND THE ONE HONEST NEXT STEP
=======================================================================
The ten refusals are now the WHOLE Z1 residual.  Four of the ten are free at
cell H.  What is not proved is that the six that remain are contradictory.

Do NOT attempt a Handelman run in this chart.  The chart is rational in the
inside atoms and in the rotation, but the outside atoms carry N^{1/2}, and the
domain in the ten parameters is cut by the positive definiteness of N and by two
eigenvalue signs -- not by linear forms.  Handelman needs a polytope and there
is none here.  The K2 route worked because the plane masses linearised its
domain; the corank-two corner has no such pair of masses, because the corner
equation is quadratic in the atoms and the weights enter through the inverse.

The route to take next is H-6, and it is now landed as machinery.  Work in the
THREE erased gaps F_4, F_5, F_6, not in the two four-set gaps of the cells.
Each F_d is positive definite whenever the axis is not coplanar with the two
surviving outside atoms, its determinant is lam times that bracket squared, and
the nine refusals are nine 2x2 sign conditions on the readings of the three
inside atoms in the three F_d metrics.  That is nine conditions on a 3x3x3 array
of readings whose diagonal is pinned by the Z1 frame -- a far smaller object
than a ten-parameter chart, and the first form of the corner residual that is
not a disjunction over which triple fires.

Also open, and cheap: the three erased gaps satisfy Parseval-type relations
among themselves, because sum_d (S_{univ\d} - 1) = 3*(S_univ - 1) - (S_univ - 1)
= 2*(S_univ - 1) + ... -- work that identity out; it prices the three metrics
against each other and no lane has looked at it.
