1. THE EXACT CORNER CHART
-------------------------
A (6,3) design with a corank-two dominator at C has an exact eleven-parameter
chart. Write the design in the basis of the inside triple. Then:

  Gram of C = 1 + lam v vT,   |v| = 1,   K := (1 + lam v vT)^-1 = 1 - mu v vT,
  mu = lam / (1 + lam),
  inside atom a  ->  coordinate vector e_a,
  outside atom d ->  coordinate vector x_d,
  Parseval       <=>  sum over d of t_d x_d x_dT = K - diag(t_1, t_2, t_3) =: X,
  a subset S dominates (strictly) <=> sum over S of xi xiT - K is PSD (PD).

The chart is (mu, v, t, Q) with Q an orthogonal matrix: put Y := X^(1/2) Q
T_out^(-1/2). Parseval and the corank-two condition then hold BY CONSTRUCTION.
Measured residuals: Parseval 1e-15, corank two 1e-16. The nine two-inside
triples and C itself refuse with no computation, exactly as the landed corner
census says. The chart is complete for X positive definite. It misses only the
boundary at which the three outside atoms are coplanar.

Source: scratchpad/cornerempty/{lin.h,chart.h,corner.c,ceil.c,law.jl,diag.py}.


2. THE CORNER AXIS IDENTITY  (new, and now in Lean)
---------------------------------------------------
For a corner C and ANY pair of labels d, e:

  sum over a in C of tripleGapDet g_d g_e g_a
     = (lam - 2) * pairGapMinor g_d g_e - (l_d + l_e - 2)
       + lam * tripleGapDet g_d g_e u.

The corner is an unweighted tight frame stretched along the axis. So the axis
enters as ONE MORE third atom, of unit leverage. Verified in C and again in an
independent Julia implementation, on the near-optimal configurations of three
weight floors, at all three outside pairs. Relative error 0 to 7e-12.

The law that follows at a tie is Gtz.corner_axisPair_law_of_isTie. The axis
minor is nonpositive at a heavy admissible pair, so the law bites only when
lam is more than two.


3. WHAT THE SEARCH SAYS
-----------------------
Minimise F = sum over the twenty triples of max(lambda_min(gap), 0)^2 over the
corner chart, with a hard weight floor tau and a hard leverage floor 1 + 1e-3.
F = 0 is exactly a tie, because the corner triple always reads lambda_min = 0.

  tau       0.05      0.02      0.005     0.0012
  margin    2.6e-3    3.7e-4    3.0e-5    5.3e-6

The margin is the largest strictly positive lambda_min over the nineteen
non-corner triples. It goes to zero with the floor. There is NO uniform
constant, and the degeneration is always the same. Two OUTSIDE atoms run to
weight tau and to leverage 0.66/tau. Their shares stay near two thirds and the
design leaves the open simplex. At every near-optimum the corner eigenvalue
sits between 2.1 and 2.8, against the threshold lam = 2 at which the law of
part 2 starts to bite.

So: the corner stratum of (6,3) carries no all-heavy tie, and a certificate for
that must consume weight positivity. Do not look for a uniform bound.


4. TWO ROUTES THAT DO NOT CLOSE THE CORNER
------------------------------------------
a) THE AREA CEILING ALONE DOES NOT. Minimising over the corner chart the
   maximum over the fifteen pairs of the ceiling violation gives -3.97. Every
   pair ceiling holds at that configuration. Recorded in scratchpad/cornerempty/ceil.c.

b) A SECOND DERIVATION OF THE CEILING, WHICH CONFIRMS BOTH. Read the pair gap
   in the frame V + R n0, with V the span of the pair and n0 the unit normal.
   Then the triple {d,e,c} dominates strictly exactly when

        (g_c . n0)^2  >  1 + g_c^T W g_c ,     W := G_V^-1 on V, 0 on n0,

   and Parseval at n0 and at W gives, with theta_d = 1 + (l_e - 1)/q_de,

        2 q (t_d + t_e) + t_d (l_e - 1) + t_e (l_d - 1)  <=  l_d + l_e - 2.

   That is ALGEBRAICALLY THE SAME as the landed Gtz.crossNormSq_le_of_isTie,
   reached from the Schur criterion instead of the third Sylvester moment. It
   is an EQUALITY at the (4,3) tetrahedron. Two independent derivations agree,
   so the ceiling is correct and it is sharp. It is not new.

The corner aggregate of part 2 is the UNWEIGHTED inside total. The ceiling is
the WEIGHTED total over all atoms. The two are independent.


5. THE K1 MINOR RATIO  (new, and now in Lean)
---------------------------------------------
At a corank-one dominator with q_ab = 0 the Sylvester chain gives the pivot row
(l_a - 1)(b.c) = (a.b)(a.c). With q_ab = 0 that is the WHOLE content: the
determinant then vanishes by itself, and the third relation at the pivot b is
implied. Two equations describe K1 completely. They force

        (l_a - 1) * q_bc = (l_b - 1) * q_ac .

So the two surviving minors are proportional, they share a sign, and one of
them vanishes only when both do. That is the trichotomy, read through a ratio.
The kernel of the gap Gram is spanned by (a.b, -(l_a - 1), 0), which is why the
third atom reads the null direction at zero.
